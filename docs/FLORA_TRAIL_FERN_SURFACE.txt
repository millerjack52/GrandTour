FloraTrail fern surface variation
=================================

The user identified the fern fans as too flat. The existing 2K pigment atlas
has RGB (58,81,25) in 99.54% of its nonblack pixels. Its visible surface is
effectively solid green. The original Fern_0_1.png contains painted grey marks,
but those marks were previously used only for opacity. The frozen fern has
414 distinct vertex normals spanning curved fronds, rather than a single shared
up-normal. Changing its geometry is not justified by this diagnosis.

Candidate
Keep the same fern mesh, all 159 instances, three LODs, source pigment,
source coverage, 0.35 cutoff, native two-sided lighting, normals, roughness,
specular and zero emissive. Import the original unblurred 2048px greyscale
artwork as a separate local texture, sampled with its existing UV0 mapping.
Use its painted values to vary pigment from 0.50 to 1.18, expanding the
near-white 0.82-0.99 range. A separate restrained growth gradient darkens the
base and shifts the tips towards warmer olive. It follows the upper-row
fronds actually addressed by this frozen mesh's UVs. It is not a generic
gradient appropriate to every fern in the pack.

These are artistic controls, not a claim that the source had a missing colour
map or that the candidate reproduces the original Blender shader. No generic
noise, generated artwork, normal-map relief, new geometry or illumination is
added. Both diffuse and subsurface use the same varied pigment, so backlighting
does not revert to the solid colour. One texture sample is added.

Scope and preservation
Only the fern component's material slot changes in a separate map:
  /Game/GrandTour/ArtTests/FloraTrail/FernSurface/L_FloraTrail_Fern
The previous UnderstoryTrial map and all 55 packages have recoverable copies
and hashes, followed by the earlier baseline chain. The comparison also checks
all 386 static-mesh component records, materials and instance transforms;
only the fern material slot is exempted. Map-local landscape paths are
canonicalized because Save As relocates those embedded objects. This caused
two early preservation-check failures; their logs are retained, not counted
as passing capture evidence.

The first fresh verification completed assertions/captures but exited with
-1073741819 during late editor shutdown. It remains a failed process result in
verify-shutdown-failed. A subsequent fresh process must pass before the launcher
is promoted; the failed run is not silently relabelled as successful.

Controls and review
  powershell -File Art/run_flora_fern_surface.ps1 -Stage candidate
  powershell -File Art/run_flora_fern_surface.ps1 -Stage verify
  python Art/review_flora_fern_surface.py

Candidate compares baseline, painted detail, growth gradient and both together
under one paused simulation with identical cameras/exposure. Views cover a
specific nearby fern, the left-hand verge and the driving view. The camera
move spans 48 frames, sampled at eight positions per control. Native captures
are 1274x682. Colour/greyscale 320px thumbnails and comparison sheets are local
under Art/Export/FloraTrail/FernSurface, stored on D: through a junction.
Fresh verification reloads the saved map and checks its material graph, source
bindings, geometry, scene signature, texture readiness and saved package hashes.

Driving physics are not retested for a fern-material-only change. The previous
UnderstoryTrial traversal remains the latest driving evidence; do not relabel
it as a new fern test. Mud and water material bindings are asserted unchanged.
No LiDAR content, tree/pine calibration, terrain or global light/grading changes
are included. Performance at sustained 60 fps remains unverified.

See validation/flora-trail-fern-surface.json for the completed visual and process
assessment. This pass addresses fern surface variation, not the remaining
whole-scene palette, canopy and atmospheric-depth requirements.

Saved result (14 November 2026)
The completed candidate comparison and repeated fresh verification both exited
zero. All three new packages retained their saved verification hashes, and
55 UnderstoryTrial packages plus the earlier baseline chain remain unchanged.
The original fern mesh, 159 instances and three LODs are preserved. The original
artwork hash matches the imported texture's recorded source hash.

Close-up surface variation improves visibly; the driving-distance change is
subtle. Broad frond shapes still read flat, so this is a partial improvement
to the user's flatness concern, not complete reference matching. Colour and
greyscale thumbnails and the sampled motion sequence are included in the review.
No new 60 fps or driving claim is made.

Launch with Drive-FloraTrail-Mud.bat. The preceding scene is available through
Drive-FloraTrail-Mud-UnderstoryBaseline.bat.
