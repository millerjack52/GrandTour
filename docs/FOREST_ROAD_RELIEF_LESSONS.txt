FOREST ROAD RELIEF — implementation and transfer notes
=====================================================

Scope
-----
Implemented first in /Game/GrandTour/ArtTests/FloraDrive/L_FloraDrive.
Production LiDAR, MudRoad, MudLab and FloraPath maps remain protected.
The companion Art/Export/FloraDrive/road-relief-review.json records the
actual validation results; do not treat this document alone as a pass.

Why the previous ground failed
-----------------------------
The close-up POM image showed texture depth, but the driving camera saw a
broad smooth surface. Soil POM was only about 1.54 cm deep, faded over
7–18 m and weakened at grazing angles. It cannot create terrain silhouettes,
tyre support or road-scale cast shadows. Normal strength was not the missing
road shape. Regular rows of loose stones reinforced the flat underlying plane.
Accepting a close-up as proof of driving-camera quality was the review error.

Implemented layers
------------------
1. Actual rest-height geometry: two wandering wheel troughs, broken crown,
   raised shoulders and irregular 25–100 cm surface forms. Keep small grains
   in the material; the native surface is sampled at approximately 12.3 cm.
2. Material variation: the same authored masks describe compacted channels
   and drier aggregate patches. Soil texture contribution is 76% rather than
   48%. Channel colour multiplier is 0.72; aggregate multiplier is up to 1.10.
   Roughness varies from 0.66 to 0.92. These are material differences, not
   baked directional shadows. Approved sunlight, exposure and palette remain.
3. Close detail: existing matching source-pack normals and 10–24-step POM.
   Height is reconstructed from normals, not supplied creator displacement.
4. Silhouette detail: grouped, partially buried instanced pack stones outside
   wheel channels. 64 interactive native clods replace 275 inherited clods.
   Decorative stones have no collision; native clods retain physical behaviour.

Single height source — essential
-------------------------------
Art/tools/road_relief_profile.py takes road station and lateral distance in cm.
Art/tools/make_flora_road_relief.py applies it to this demo's authored bed.
The resulting world-rest.png is imported into GTLandscapeSurface CPU rest
heights AND bound to GTLS_Rest in local rendering/water materials. Updating
only one causes floating tyres, incorrect water depth or visual/physics mismatch.
The existing live tyre deformation remains layered onto the authored rest bed.
Reset must return to the new authored bed, not flatten it back to zero.

The present encoding is RG16 with range -50..+15 cm. This pass clamps authored
road heights to -42..+14 cm and validates the soil thickness bound. These are
not general landscape elevation limits: the rest field is a local offset from
the base terrain. Do not put metres of landscape relief into this encoding.
Numeric textures need sRGB off; the packed rest field is uncompressed numeric
data with no mipmaps. Colour textures and the material control mask have their
own appropriate sampling settings. Do not copy colour-import settings to rest.

Reproduce the isolated demo
---------------------------
From C:/GrandTour, with no other Unreal or Blender process using the slot:
  python Art/tools/make_flora_drive_pond.py
  python Art/tools/make_flora_road_relief.py
  powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File Art/run_flora_drive.ps1 -Stage build
  powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File Art/run_flora_drive.ps1 -Stage check
  [After a clean successful check, archive check-report.json as functional-report.json.]
  powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File Art/run_flora_drive.ps1 -Stage capture
  python Art/tools/review_flora_road_relief.py
The pond authoring resets the base first: applying road deltas repeatedly to
an already modified bed would accumulate them. The scatter seed is fixed.

Transfer to actual game maps
---------------------------
* Use the actual road spline to compute closest station/lateral coordinates.
  Replace this demo's sine-road adapter and hard-coded shader world bounds.
* Fit trough spacing to vehicle track width; the fixture uses +/-83 cm centres
  with 39 cm trough half-width and small lateral wander. Crown, shoulder,
  depth, footprint, seed, tile origin/extent and entry/exit blending belong
  in per-map authoring settings before broad deployment.
* Blend offsets into the existing LiDAR height, keeping junctions, bridges,
  hard surfaces and stream crossings explicitly masked. Water placement must
  be checked again after any roadbed edit; lowered ground can reveal water.
* Export rest data and material masks together. Bind actual assigned material
  instances and their parents, not a similarly named unused parent material.
* Derive scatter height from the same terrain, bury stones, preserve wheels'
  usable channels, and keep colliders only where gameplay needs them.
* Review dry, damp and submerged stretches plus slope transitions. Plants
  must root into the resulting surface rather than retain the old height.
* Trial one representative production road section before map-wide generation.

Acceptance gate for every map
----------------------------
VISUAL: full-size chase-camera before/after at fixed recovery points, plus a
fixed world camera. Keep lighting, exposure, resolution and lens unchanged.
Chase camera may settle slightly differently on new terrain; disclose this.
Confirm broad relief remains visible with POM disabled. Close-ups supplement
this check; they cannot replace it. Inspect moving footage in the map-wide
rollout for shimmer, LOD changes, clipping and wet-surface artefacts.
FUNCTIONAL: continuous route drive, recovery spawns, suspension contact,
live compression, water depth and reset to authored heights. Shader-oracle
checks must still pass. A completed report with a crashing process is not a
clean automation pass; investigate and record retries separately.
PERFORMANCE: compare same-resolution, warmed, uncapped views without capture
or pause inside the timing window. Record GPU and frame medians/p95, counts
and hardware contention. Existing grid geometry is reused; one mask sample
and additional instanced stones still cost something. No claim of a free
effect or packaged 60 FPS from an editor spot check.

Review provenance
-----------------
BeforeRoadRelief contains the preserved previous captures and authored data.
All comparison views are rendered by Unreal. No generated replacement images.
See road-relief-review.html and road-relief-review.json for the current result.

Validation lesson from this pass
-------------------------------
The first functional run traversed the route but failed the 0.5 cm parked-tyre
sink comparison on reset. It compared 16.48 seconds of fresh-world simulation
against 11.93 seconds after reset. The car also settled several centimetres
away on the soft entry. The test now waits for matching simulated age before
comparing; its thresholds and exact world-space reset-height check are retained.
The rerun passed with 51.4 m driven, 9.47 cm maximum compression, 26.06 cm
maximum water depth and zero reset-height error. Five native shader oracle
errors were all below 0.00060, against the existing 0.005 tolerance.
The failed report and shutdown exit are retained separately; the passing
rerun finished with process exit zero. Do not overwrite failure evidence.

Final visual capture also exited cleanly. The runtime crown-to-trough minimum
across the eight checked sections was 16.33 cm. Warming and sampling 300 frames
per view at 1274x682 gave GPU medians 30.89 ms (road) and 30.90 ms (pond), versus
32.43 / 32.62 ms in the saved previous pass. This observation shows no regression
in these samples; it does not isolate individual costs or establish a general
speedup. Frame timing remains variable, and a packaged performance pass is
still required before broad map deployment.
