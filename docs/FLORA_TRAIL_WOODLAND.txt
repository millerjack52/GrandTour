FloraTrail woodland candidate / 15 November 2026
================================================

Decision
Keep the original 159 Flora Paint ferns, including their existing material.
Woodland v3 is a separate, reviewable art-direction candidate. It is not a
reference-matched result and is not approved for LiDAR rollout. Its palette
is more coherent, but crown modelling by light and the procedural-looking
rock distribution still fall short of the supplied woodland references.

Map: /Game/GrandTour/ArtTests/FloraTrail/Woodland/v3/L_FloraTrail_Woodland
Baseline: /Game/GrandTour/ArtTests/FloraTrail/FernSurface/L_FloraTrail_Fern
The existing default launchers still open their previous maps.

What changed
45 component material slots use map-local copies: four canopy, four bark,
three shrub, one grass, one litter and 32 rock slots. Source textures, UVs,
meshes, LOD counts, component transforms and every instance transform remain
unchanged. Original ferns, landscape, deformable mud and flat water retain
their original material bindings. No LiDAR, isolated source-calibration or
shared source asset changes are part of this candidate.

Canopy pigment is cooler green, with restrained variation by instance.
World-up normals blend toward signed mesh normals at 0.78; native two-sided
foliage shading uses restrained subsurface colour (12% of base), roughness
0.82 and specular 0.04. This is an artistic candidate, not a claim to reproduce
Blender's shader. Camera-facing coverage uses the original coverage texture
with a facing term; shadow passes retain the source coverage expression.
This reduces fine visible stipple but leaves several large opaque-looking
clusters. Geometry was not rebuilt to conceal that remaining failure.

Ground plant families repeat coral/ochre in whole clumps, not random leaves.
Bark receives a restrained cooler tint. Rocks retain their detailed source
textures, with mineral tones moderated and green-dominant moss redirected
toward olive. The large grey rock still reads separately in the road view;
texture correctness does not make that a visual pass.

Sun energy remains 16 (native 15.68), with its original direction restored.
Sun colour is [1,0.89,0.69]; cool sky strength 1.3 (native 1.274), colour
[0.55,0.72,0.90]. Sun source angle is 1.2. Exposure remains zero; the existing
Standard display transform is retained. No global desaturation, blur,
emissive foliage or new final colour grade was added.

Depth was adjusted after the material/light comparisons: fog density 0.045,
height falloff 0.16, start distance 1200 cm, maximum opacity 0.65; volumetric
extinction 0.65, scattering distribution 0.35, start distance 1000 cm, near
fade 1400 cm; fog colour [0.22,0.34,0.40], sun scattering 0.22.

Controlled evidence and causes
The prior independent pigment/normal/neutral-light/azimuth/display tests are
documented in FLORA_TRAIL_PALETTE_DIAGNOSIS.txt. They found that world-up normals
contribute to uniformly filled crowns, while neither tint alone nor neutral
illumination restores source form. The Standard midgrey test passed; it is
not a complete display-curve characterization. This pass preserves that
display transform rather than introducing a compensating global grade.

Three candidate iterations are retained locally. V1, with lower sunlight and
direct texture coverage in all passes, made the road excessively dark and
crowns too solid. V2 restored source shadow coverage and the original sun
direction, improving ground light; its crowns and moss remained too lime.
V3 tests native two-sided canopy shading and a separate moss-colour treatment.
Those two changes concern different material families; this is not a claim
that their individual contributions have been isolated by a single metric.

V3 captures hold camera, FOV, display, exposure and quality fixed across:
baseline; family materials/normals; camera coverage; lighting; final depth;
and reverse sun azimuth. Five views cover road, mixed bank, lower canopy,
upward crowns and an elevated overview. Reverse light covers the first three.
Baseline and final each include eight samples from a short 48-step camera
move. All game captures are native 1274 x 682, not upscaled evidence. Shaders
and asynchronous textures are awaited, including 4K bark and the pine mask.
No per-camera exposure or grading fixes are used.

The elevated view is a local test-section overview, not a LiDAR valley.
The under-canopy view has foreground, middle and distant layers, but it is
still an open woodland composition rather than the reference's forest corridor.

Visual gates (partial does not count as passed)
A PALETTE: PARTIAL. Green/olive replaces much of the uniform yellow response,
  and coral/ochre recurs through the bank. Cream accents and coloured shade
  remain less convincing than the reference. See before-after/reference panels.
B CROWN FORM: FAIL. Three crown crops and 320px grey views show variation, but
  some foreground and elevated crowns still read as flat opaque leaf masses.
  The native foliage variant reduces lime brightness without fully recovering
  connected light-facing planes and intermediate volume. See semantic-crops.
C SHADE: PARTIAL. Ferns, shrubs and trunks remain identifiable in shade;
  cooler colour separation is weak and several overlapping clusters flatten.
  See lit/shaded fern and bark crops and reverse-light bank.
D MATERIAL COHESION: PARTIAL. Bark, plants and small moss rocks share warmer
  sunlight more consistently. Large grey rocks and the repeated roadside
  pebble distribution remain conspicuous. See mixed bank and road.
E DEPTH: PASS for the tested lower-canopy composition. Near fern/contact
  detail survives, middle trunks separate, and far forest loses contrast
  into cooler haze. This does not certify an enclosed forest or LiDAR valley.
F TWO SCALES / MOTION: PARTIAL. Colour groups read at thumbnail size and source
  detail remains close up. The eight motion samples retain their palette, but
  foliage coverage remains coarse and this sampled move cannot certify all
  temporal shimmer, wind or vehicle-speed behaviour. No blur was used.
G CONSISTENCY: FAIL. All required local views, reverse lighting and elevated
  regression views were captured, but B fails and A/C/D/F are incomplete.
Performance: NOT TESTED. Offscreen capture timing is not sustained 60 fps.
Driving/reset regression: NOT TESTED in this pass. The same existing review
strokes exercise the visible mud/water state; earlier physical driving results
are not re-labelled as a new traversal test.

Verification and recovery
The runner protects prior baselines before and after each run and refuses to
start beside another Unreal/Blender renderer or below its RAM/disk thresholds.
No unrelated application is terminated. Baseline package hashes and the local
backup are checked. Candidate material/level hashes must remain unchanged
during capture; source texture bindings, mesh/instance data and fern bindings
are asserted after save/reload. The verify stage opens a fresh editor process,
audits saved light/fog/exposure before PIE, and does not reapply lighting.
Runtime post-process bias fields are audited only when their override is set;
an unused default bias of 1 is not an active exposure override.
See validation/flora-trail-woodland.json for actual final run results.

Reproduce from C:/GrandTour, one renderer at a time:
  powershell -File Art/run_flora_woodland.ps1 -Stage candidate
  powershell -File Art/run_flora_woodland.ps1 -Stage verify
  python Art/review_flora_woodland.py
Settings: Art/flora_woodland_settings.json. Material versions are immutable;
increment revision before changing material construction to avoid stale reuse.
Do not merge this candidate into shared source materials or LiDAR.
