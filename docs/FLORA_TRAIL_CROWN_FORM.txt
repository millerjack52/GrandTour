FloraTrail: directional crown shading / 15 November 2026
=======================================================

Request
Improve the flat crowns and their lighting variation while keeping the
accepted browner mud and the rest of the woodland fixed.

Baseline
/Game/GrandTour/ArtTests/FloraTrail/Woodland/BrownMud/L_FloraTrail_BrownMud
This map and its two mud materials are backed up and hash-protected, together
with the preceding Woodland v3 and earlier baseline chain. Shared source
assets, the Blender/Unreal calibration bench and LiDAR remain unchanged.

Implemented controls
Only canopy material slots are replaced with separate material copies.
Source pigment/coverage textures, UVs, geometry, instance placement and LODs
are retained. Bark, original ferns, shrubs, rocks, terrain, mud and water keep
their saved materials. Sun, skylight, exposure, display and fog stay fixed.
The reverse-light diagnostic temporarily reverses sun azimuth in PIE; that
direction is not saved. No emissive foliage, blur or global grade is added.

The previous world-space normal blended world-up with VertexNormalWS times
TwoSidedSign. This makes part of the lighting normal depend on which side of
the card the camera sees. The installed UE5.8 MaterialTemplate.ush applies its
backface-normal sign only to tangent-space normals (or the special single-layer
water path), not this world-space foliage output. The new enabled branch
removes that camera-facing sign from the mesh normal.

An ellipsoid normal, centred at 62% of local mesh height, is calculated from
pre-skinned local position and per-mesh bounds. Instance-to-world conversion
keeps independently rotated tree instances aligned with the actual sun.
This describes the crown's broad lit face and shaded side. A second normal
curves each foliage fan using its existing UV0 and tangent basis; it describes
smaller overlapping surfaces. These are shading normals, not a mesh rebuild
or a claim to recover the source author's exact normal construction.

The final iteration also tests a local ambient-occlusion approximation:
interior foliage receives less sky fill, with exposed sides/tops and fan edges
remaining more open. It feeds the native Ambient Occlusion output; the source
base colour remains unchanged. It is an authored volume/fan approximation,
not newly ray-traced AO or a reproduction of Blender GTAO.

Controls live in Art/flora_crown_settings.json. Versioned material paths keep
prior iterations recoverable. Increase revision for graph-construction changes.
Scalar defaults can be changed within a candidate revision and saved explicitly.

Controlled comparison
V1 tested the original shader, removal of the camera-facing sign, whole-crown
normals, and a coverage trim. Whole-crown normals produced broad top/side
variation, but individual fans remained sheet-like. Trimming opacity primarily
changed edges and was not sufficient to correct lighting.
V2 added curved fan normals and combined them with crown normals. Its captures
completed and reported their assertions passed, but Unreal then returned
0xC0000005 during shutdown after the log closed. This is retained as a failed
process run, not a technical pass. Optional shader-statistics queries were
removed from the verification harness; that does not establish the crash cause.
V3 additionally separates occlusion-only, normals-only and combined cases.
The complete review includes road, mixed bank, lower canopy, upward crowns,
elevated overview and reversed-light captures, with no per-camera corrections.
Baseline and final include eight sampled frames from a short camera move.

Verification
The saved map is reloaded before comparison. A fresh verify process reads
stored settings without installing or setting crown parameters at runtime.
Texture bindings, native texture sizes, mesh/instance data, other material
bindings, light/fog/exposure settings, brown mud and flat water are checked.
Candidate hashes must remain unchanged during captures. Renderer exit status,
shader-error logs and protected-baseline hashes are checked by the runner.
Native image size is 1274 x 682; comparison layouts do not increase resolution.
The sampled motion sequence is not real-time footage or a 60 fps measurement.

Selected result
CrownForm/v3 uses enabled=1, crown volume blend=0.5, curved fan blend=1,
interior occlusion strength=0.9, coverage trim=0. The original silhouette and
coverage are therefore retained. Main gains come from corrected/curved normals;
AO adds a smaller, local reduction in shaded sky fill. The side/top transitions
are clearer in the road-edge and conifer crops and in 320px grey thumbnails.
Some near fans remain dense and sheet-like. Strong frontal/reversed sunlight
still produces overly broad yellow-green faces. This is a better selected
daylight candidate, not a completed reference match or a source-parity claim.

Visual gates
A PARTIAL: local warm highlights are more distinct; cream accents and cool
  interiors still fall short of the reference. See colour thumbnails.
B PARTIAL (previously fail): three crown crops now show clearer light/shade
  separation, but dense near fans and the reverse-light view remain too flat.
C PARTIAL: shaded forms retain green/blue-green colour; overlapping interiors
  remain less differentiated than the references. See canopy/crowns views.
D PARTIAL: trees use the unchanged native light; the previous mixed-material
  weaknesses, particularly large grey rocks, remain. See bank.
E PASS in the tested open under-canopy composition: foreground detail, middle
  trunks and cooler distant layers remain separated. Not a LiDAR validation.
F PARTIAL: crown value structure improves at 320px and source detail remains
  close up; sampled motion retains shading, but coverage still looks coarse.
  The sample sequence does not certify all temporal shimmer or wind behaviour.
G FAIL: reverse lighting still exposes broad bright fills; other partial gates
  prevent an overall pass. All five views and reverse light were reviewed.
Performance and new physical driving regression: NOT TESTED.
