FLORATRAIL MUD MATERIAL COMPARISON - 14 NOVEMBER 2026
==================================================

Decision
Brown Mud 02 is the leading wet-road candidate. Its irregular aggregates catch
the existing light and preserve a subdued soil colour. It improves close and
driving-height surface texture without making the entire road orange.
This is a material-selection result, not acceptance of the complete art style.

Mud Forest is the useful second choice: its green/ochre leaf litter connects
with woodland colours, but scattered leaves over the entire worked road are
less convincing than using it on unworked edges. That blend is not installed.
Stylized Dry Mud 001 has the strongest graphic cracks and a warm colour, but
reads as baked crust beside standing water. Reserve it for dry areas.
Muddy Tracks gives directional clay detail, but its red/orange base dominates
this scene and its fine corrugation is less legible than Brown Mud's clods.

Controlled comparison
Four fixed cameras: driving height, close oblique road surface, farther road,
and the established forest-path/context camera. Same camera positions, FOV 65,
render settings, lighting and exposure within each comparison run. Actual
screenshots are 1274 x 682, despite a requested 1600 x 900 PIE window.
Two identical native soil strokes precede the captures. Simulation is then
paused while the camera controller continues ticking, fixing water/soil/wind
state across candidates. The parked vehicle is hidden for the final fixed
camera review, then restored for the physical drive.

Each candidate has a 48-frame camera move with eight saved samples over 2.1 m.
The WebP animations are sampled previews, not realtime frame-rate evidence.
The 1K screen_* pass was exploratory; its farther view was obstructed by the
parked vehicle and its scalar import format was superseded. Use the corrected
native_* captures and native-report.json as the final comparison evidence.

Material implementation and diagnosed import causes
The road-only wrapper retains the source material's native deformation/WPO,
normal contribution, terrain inputs and water-related bindings. Candidate
base colour, normal, height and roughness are added in a feathered road mask.
Source pigment is ungraded; every candidate receives the same existing wetness
modulation. Roughness combines 65% candidate and 35% existing road response.
AO maps are retained in the source inventory but are not used by this wrapper.

POM supplies 2.8 cm of visual height relief, fading between 8 and 18 m. It does
not add collision or new terrain silhouettes. Existing native ruts/banks do
that work. Normal strength is 1. Source tile widths: Brown Mud 02 130 cm,
Muddy Tracks 225 cm, Mud Forest 235 cm. The stylized pack specifies no physical
tile width; its provisional test width is 200 cm. Equal height range is a
comparison convention, not a claim that the scans share a calibrated depth.

The stylized normal's Y convention is opposite to the Poly Haven DirectX maps.
Normal XY versus height-gradient checks verify the orientation; only the
stylized normal receives a green-channel flip on Unreal import.

Native 16-bit PNG colour imports for Muddy Tracks and Mud Forest appeared
substantially brighter than the source/1K copies. Final imports use explicit
8-bit PNG copies at the original pixel dimensions, with sRGB colour and
linear normal/scalar data. Scalar 16-bit values are divided by 257, not clipped
into an 8-bit image. No source hue adjustment is involved. Raw downloaded
files and their hashes are retained on D:/GrandTourAssets/MudTextureTrial.
Poly Haven inputs are 4K; the free stylized pack is 1K. No 4K claim is made for
the latter. Shader/texture loading and temporal settling precede judgement.

Visual results and limits
Close surface relief: pass for Brown Mud; clear irregular highlights/shading.
Driving-height read: pass for the nearby road; materially more grain/clods
than the baseline. Original-resolution imports are needed for a fair close
comparison; a successful texture binding alone is not visual acceptance.
Farther read: partial. Broad road relief remains clear, but fine material
features merge into tone with distance. These maps do not independently solve
the user's requirement for large readable ground roughness at long range.
Palette fit: partial. Brown Mud is restrained; Mud Forest supplies stronger
litter accents. The entire scene still lacks the reference's material cohesion.
Motion: partial. Sampled camera sequences retain the ground pattern and broad
forms; this is not a full temporal-noise or performance certification. The
existing foliage still has a conspicuously noisy edge/detail structure.

FLORA_PALETTE_SUCCESS_CRITERIA gates for this limited mud study:
A fail: uniform yellow-green crown regions and weak vegetation accent structure
  remain; changing road texture has not repaired the palette hierarchy.
B partial: connected crown light/shade exists, but some crowns remain flat and
  noisy in the fixed-view thumbnails. No new three-crown lighting sweep.
C partial: shaded ground and trunks retain colour; much understory remains
  dark and poorly separated. No dedicated lit/shaded fern crop acceptance.
D fail: new soil receives the same sunlight, but grey rocks and dark ground
  plants still do not form a cohesive reference-like material ensemble.
E not tested: these ground comparisons do not establish the required three
  forest depth layers. The preceding failed scene assessment is not superseded.
F partial: broad road shapes survive thumbnail reduction and surface detail
  survives close viewing. Overall painterly shape/temporal acceptance remains.
G not tested: no new complete front/side/back-light and valley regression set.
  Prior scene failures remain unresolved; this is not a LiDAR rollout approval.
Sustained 60 fps: not tested. POM and extra texture sampling have a cost; this
study does not establish a performance budget for production or LiDAR.

Reproduction
Art/prepare_flora_mud_trial.py retains/downloads the public Poly Haven sources,
creates normalized import copies, and checks normal/height orientation. The
stylized public Drive file IDs are in that script; place those free files in
D:/GrandTourAssets/MudTextureTrial/stylized_dry_mud before running it.
Art/run_flora_mud_trial.ps1 -Stage screen creates the isolated wrapper/map.
-Stage native compares all candidates at their original pixel dimensions.
-Stage saved installs the selection in Art/flora_mud_trial_settings.json,
reloads the map, verifies its inherited texture parameters without runtime
overrides, captures it, and performs a continuous physical road traversal.
-Stage verify reloads the saved selection in a fresh process without editing
assets or overriding the runtime material parameters.
Art/review_flora_mud_trial.py creates comparison figures from completed runs.
The runner checks disk, memory, renderer processes, protected hashes and logs.
It never terminates unrelated applications. Only one renderer runs at a time.
