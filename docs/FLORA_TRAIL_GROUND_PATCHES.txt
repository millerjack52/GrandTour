Scope and diagnosis
The approved tree/undergrowth palette, CrownForm/v3 shading, brown wet mud and
flat water are the baseline. The exposed verge has a pale moss strip and even
fine mottling. Small green stones form conspicuous road-edge bands. The woodland
reference instead has connected soil/moss/litter areas and irregular plant and
rock groups. This first comparison isolates surface distribution; it does not
change stone or plant placement, and does not claim to solve those bands.

Implementation
Separate map/material copies under
/Game/GrandTour/ArtTests/FloraTrail/Woodland/GroundPatches/v2.
The existing forest-floor wrapper in terrain and native overlay is extended
with GroundPatchStrength. Current moss/litter weights blend toward larger
irregular fields: 680 cm moss, 430 cm litter, 145 cm coordinate warp driven
by a 240 cm field. Moss and litter share the coordinate field; litter coverage
is restrained in strong moss regions. This avoids replacing the entire floor
with one continuous green layer. Existing source texture tiling is retained.

Profiles: baseline 0; patches .75; strong 1. Selected candidate is patches .75.
Source albedo and normal samples, 2K resolution, original tint, roughness and

remain exactly the existing expressions. No new texture reads, POM settings,
mud deformation, water bindings, lights, exposure or postprocess effects.
No mesh, LOD, transform, instance count, plant material or rock material changes.
The calibrated source bench, LiDAR and earlier baseline packages are protected.

Initial visual assessment
The .75 variant replaces some pale moss strip with warmer soil and connected
litter. The change is modest and most visible beside the road and under nearby
plants. Fine texture survives; it is not a new painted texture or geometry pass.
The full-strength variant adds more of that distribution but offers no clear
reason to prefer it. The repeated bright green stones and isolated small plant
clumps still dominate the composition. Those are the next ground-art priority;
global darkening, desaturation or more texture noise would not remove repetition.
