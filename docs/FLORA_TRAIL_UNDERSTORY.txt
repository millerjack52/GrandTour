FloraTrail: left-hand plants and moss-rock detail
================================================

Scope
The user's left-hand patch is the actual grass/undergrowth, rather than the
exposed ground treated in the preceding verge pass. This candidate changes only
the four grass/dry-grass/fern/shrub components and twelve small Flora Paint rock
mesh families in a separate map:
  /Game/GrandTour/ArtTests/FloraTrail/UnderstoryTrial/L_FloraTrail_Understory

Causes and changes
The old undergrowth conversion used the Blender 5 transmission approximation:
weighted pigment was squared, the upper-hemisphere reflection contribution was
gated off, and facing coverage was passed through a temporal screen-space hash.
Trees had subsequently received the calibrated Blender 3.4 response; these
plants had not. The resulting undergrowth was both dark and perforated.

Three plant controls isolate coverage, light response, and their combination.
Coverage uses the same source mask/UV0 with a 0.35 cutoff, without random holes.
Lighting uses native two-sided foliage, the original pigment/UV1 at 0.45 diffuse
gain and 0.15 subsurface gain, vertex normals, roughness 0.8 and specular 0.08.
Emissive output is zero. This is a visual candidate, not a measured claim of
Blender parity. Grass, fern, shrub and flower source images remain unmodified.
All plant meshes, instance counts and their existing LODs remain unchanged.

The small moss rocks had no surface texture: the original procedural colour
was baked only to vertices, including rocks with just 34 triangles. Increasing
texture streaming quality could not recover detail that was absent. The source
procedural pigment is now baked in Blender 3.4.1 to individual 2048px UV1 atlases.
UV0, source shape/triangle counts, normals and one rock LOD are preserved in new
local meshes. BC7 sRGB colour is sampled with normal mipmapping. The authored
rock diffuse shader has no connected normal input; unused bump nodes are not
invented as an extra surface layer. Roughness/specular remain 0.94/0.04.

Preservation
L_FloraTrail_Verge and all 47 previous MudTrial packages are backed up and checked
by hash, in addition to the earlier source/tree/pine/LiDAR baseline chain.
The accepted pine and tree materials, 4K bark, scene sunlight, skylight,
exposure, display transform, fog, woodland floor, wet mud and flat-water
materials remain unchanged. Do not promote this material pass into LiDAR.

Reproduction
With all Unreal/Blender renderers closed and sufficient disk/RAM, bake using
Blender 3.4.1 with Art/Blender/bake_flora_rock_detail.py. Never save over the
licensed blend. The exporter verifies its source hash and triangle counts.
Then run sequentially from C:/GrandTour:
  powershell -File Art/run_flora_understory.ps1 -Stage candidate
  powershell -File Art/run_flora_understory.ps1 -Stage verify
  python Art/review_flora_understory.py

Candidate stages baseline, coverage-only, lighting-only, rock-detail-only and
combined under the same paused simulation, cameras and exposure. It explicitly
asserts successful mesh swaps; temporary PIE mobility changes are restored.
Verification reloads the saved map in a fresh process, checks source bindings,
LODs/counts and texture readiness, captures seven views plus a short camera move,
then runs the existing physical mud/water traversal. Native captures are
1274x682; review boards are reduced displays, never claimed as higher resolution.

Evidence is under Art/Export/FloraTrail/UnderstoryStudy (local media on D: through
a junction). The first comparison is retained in invalid-static-mesh-swap and
rejected: Unreal refused static mesh replacement during PIE, so its rock
baseline used the wrong mesh/vertex-colour pairing. Subsequent captures must
pass the actual mesh assertion. Render/process failures are recorded separately
from visual acceptance; a successful screenshot is not a reference match.

Limitations
Solid coverage intentionally changes the softness of the source hashed blend.
Some grass cards and fern fans can still look flat; the moss tops remain bright.
This pass does not fix the complete forest palette, canopy shape, composition,
fog depth or selective lighting. The new atlases add texture memory. Sustained
60 fps, a valley view and front/side/back-light sweeps remain unverified.
See validation/flora-trail-understory.json for the final evidence and gate review.

Saved result (14 November 2026)
Candidate comparison and fresh verification both exited zero. All 55 candidate
packages and the protected earlier packages retained their verification hashes.
The four plant components hold 9,702 instances and retain three LODs. The twelve
rock families hold 1,015 instances and retain their original triangle counts
and single LOD. All sampled source/rock textures were resident at 2048x2048.
The continuous traversal covered 51.72 m, reached 20.30 cm wheel water depth,
and measured 5.77 cm maximum ground-height change. Native ground shading and
water-bed updates remained active. This is not a new reset/performance test.

The completed review records A fail, B-F partial and G not tested. Local rock
detail and plant coverage improve; complete reference matching is not achieved.
Launch with Drive-FloraTrail-Mud.bat; recover the previous accepted ground and
water scene with Drive-FloraTrail-Mud-VergeBaseline.bat.
