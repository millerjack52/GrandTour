## Changes

- `UGTDrivetrainComponent::BeginPlay` initializes each Ferrari movement instance before tuning captures its base: 450 Nm, 900 RPM idle, 7,500 RPM limit, the previously authored Ferrari torque curve, ratios 2.90/2.25/1.58/1.18/0.92, final drive 4.10, 7,000/2,700 RPM shifts, 0.22 s shift cut and 2:1 low range. The taller first gear makes powered exits more progressive. This covers fresh and placed cars without rewriting map instances or shared component defaults.
- The tested engine/clutch helpers moved to `GTIndependentDrivetrain.h`. Range Rover aliases and default parameters preserve its behavior. Ferrari uses 0.38 kg m² engine/flywheel inertia and 0.30 s engagement. Handbrake opens actual drive; release reconnects progressively. The simplified hybrid clutch retains the prior back-drive speed constraint.
- Ferrari RPM uses the driven rear axle. Shift hold considers driven-wheel contact and road-versus-shaft RPM. The rear differential conserves input torque, with limited-slip coupling of 0.25 on full power easing to 0.0375 on lift. Unpowered front wheels receive no drive or lock coupling. Moving transfer shifts are rejected.
- Only the dedicated Ferrari wheel assets change: front/rear springs 320/350, damping 0.62/0.66, travel 8 cm raise/12 cm drop, front steering lock 35°. Tyre friction, mass, steering input filters, shared wheel classes and graphics retain their existing settings.
- Ferrari-only physics steering increases ordinary high-range steering travel by 25% through the speed curve, capped at full lock. It additionally blends out the speed-dependent travel reduction when the driver's filtered input points into an existing sideslip. This countersteer blend runs from 3° to 10° of sideslip and 20–45 km/h, retains the existing inner/outer wheel-angle geometry and the authored 35° lock, and is disabled on release, normal turn-in, reverse and low range. Low range bypasses both scale changes. This changes available wheel angle, not steering direction or chassis yaw torque. The original tyre-force model remains responsible for catching the slide.
- `-GTFerrariTest` selects a transient Ferrari starting pawn in the lab; F1 shows gear/RPM and LSD state. The saved rally map remains unchanged.
