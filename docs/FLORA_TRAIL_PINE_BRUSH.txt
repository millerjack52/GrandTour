Observation and hypothesis
The original 4K SourceCoverage atlas contains painted streaks and rough edges.
It contains multiple shapes mapped through UV0; the separate pigment atlas
uses UV1. No replacement texture is required to begin this experiment.
Current camera coverage is A*(.15+.85*abs(N dot V)^.7), clipped at .35.
That threshold and angle response may suppress useful brush character, but
this is not yet demonstrated. Higher thresholds can also destroy thin strokes.
Removing angle attenuation can make crowns denser. Either may be rejected.

Prepared probes (same source texture sampling and existing mips)
- baseline: exact inherited CrownForm/v3 output.
- threshold45: change only the camera coverage threshold to .45.
- threshold55: change only the camera coverage threshold to .55.
- fixed45: .45 threshold, remove camera-angle attenuation; compare to threshold45.
The original shadow-depth branch is returned unchanged in every probe.
No temporal dither, pigment noise, emissive, sharpen, blur or new colour tint.
Material clip stays .35; output offset implements the tested threshold.
These are diagnostic probes, not a claim that a threshold can create new
brush strokes or solve broad branch shape and normal problems.
