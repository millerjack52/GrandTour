# 2026-10-08: mud leaves the bog (MudRunner MR1)

The first of the MudRunner lessons: the world reacts to the mud.

- **Muddy tracks out of the bogs.** A wheel picks mud up in a bog (full in a second or two) and, back on firm ground, leaves it
  behind as broken clumps that thin out: half the load is gone every 15 m, and water washes it off in a couple of metres. The
  tracks fade over ten minutes and dry from dark and glossy to lighter and matte in about a minute and a half.
- **Thrown mud lands.** Mud flung by a spinning wheel that comes down outside the bog now leaves a splat on the ground, or a
  muddy splat on a post or a rock it hits, instead of vanishing.
- **Muddy grass.** Tussocks over the carried mud get dark mud at their feet with a few flecks above.
- **New art**: sixteen painterly mud blots, splatters and drag smears baked in Blender (no two alike, no lines or swirls), plus
  a tileable splatter of droplets and drips for the muddy car (MR2).
- **A bug from the variants stage fixed**: the two mud strips' ground was far too shiny away from the bogs (the tussocks were
  mirrored in the gravel); it reads matte again.
- **Seen**: in one picture the tracks run out of the Stony bog as two thin dotted lines of small specks; I've made the clumps
  bigger since (about a tyre wide), but God of War was running for the later drives and the car never got that far, so the new
  size is unseen. A post at the edge of the Deep bog took two mud splats from a spin-up; with the game running, a splat once drew
  as a black square. The muddy grass hasn't been seen in a picture yet.
- **Tests**: a new mud-carry test passes (the pick-up, the shedding, the washing, the broken trail, the fading and drying),
  with every other mud and snow test unchanged. No speed or frame-cost measurements were taken. Each part has an on/off switch
  for you to compare: `gt.Mud.Carry`, `gt.Mud.Splats`, `gt.Mud.GrassMud`.

# 2026-10-08 (later): a muddy car (MudRunner MR2), and MR1 polished

The second MudRunner lesson: the car gets muddy the way the trucks do.

- **Tyres cake in mud** in a second or two, treads first, then the sidewalls, then the rims; the mud turns with the wheels and
  stays until water or rain washes it off.
- **A mud line that rises with how deep you sank**: drive through a bog and the body is coated up to the mud's depth plus a
  bit, higher still if you spin the wheels. The line is splattered and uneven - it rises in tongues and rides higher round the
  wheels - with droplets and short drips thrown above it, thinning out over most of a metre. It stays when you leave the mud:
  dried mud stays until it is washed.
- **Spinning flings mud** up the arches and onto the bumper of the spinning axle. Mud thrown at the body coats where it lands
  (the only way mud gets onto glass), and mud thrown at the floor coats it and drops back into the bog.
- **Wet and dry**: fresh mud is dark and glossy and dries lighter and matte over a minute and a half.
- **Washing**: drive through a ford and everything below the waterline comes clean, tyres included, while the mud above it
  stays. A bog's rut water doesn't wash (it's slurry). Rain washes everything, slowly.
- **The wheels have a material at last** (they were the engine's grey grid): dark rubber and a grey rim, which is what the mud
  cakes.
- **MR1 polished**: the carried tracks no longer read as dotted lines (the clumps overlap now and the trail breaks into smears
  and gaps; the ground material had been turning a continuous track into specks); the muddy grass works (it was measuring the
  grass from the wrong place, and its mud was the same brown as the straw); the black square on the post is gone (a mud splat
  now waits until its material is ready instead of showing the engine's placeholder); the thrown-mud checks are far fewer.
- **Seen**: from the side and the back the car is coated dark brown below a line that rises and falls in tongues, with droplets and
  drips thrown above it and the tyres caked; after L_MudDrive's ford the bottom of the doors and the tyres are clean while the
  mud above the waterline stays (the waterline itself is a fairly level edge). The carried tracks out of a bog now run as dark
  smears that fade into ragged traces in a clean run; with God of War open the strip drive stalls, so the pictures on disk come
  from a scripted pass, and there the tracks look like loose trails of separate blots. The muddy grass shows as darker blade
  feet in a clean run. The terrain test failed twice on its known flaky step (the held exit out of the ruts), also with the
  muddy car switched off.
- **Tests**: a new muddy-car test passes (tyres, sills, roof, spin by axle, washing below the waterline only, drying in 90 s,
  rain), and every other mud and snow test is unchanged. No speed or frame-cost measurements were taken. One new on/off
  switch to compare: `gt.Mud.CarMud`.

# 2026-10-08 (later): splatter with structure on the muddy car (MudRunner MR2b)

The first look at the muddy car found two faults from the owner's rejection list: the top of the mud was a row of rounded
tongues with straight vertical sides (a city skyline), and the droplets above it were spread evenly over the doors like polka
dots. Both are fixed; the caked tyres and the coat below stay.

- **The mud line is one sloped, jagged edge along each side**: low in the middle of the doors, rising about 15 cm over each
  wheel arch, never steeper than about 1.5 (no vertical walls), its edge broken into small jags by a fine noise and the
  splatter's own droplets. The old line came from a top-down sample of the bog that didn't change up the side of the car,
  which is what drew the vertical walls.
- **Each wheel throws its own fan of spray**: a wheel spinning or rolling through mud builds up a fan of droplets that opens up
  and back from its arch (forward if the wheel spins backward for over a second), densest right behind the arch and thinning
  over about a metre; elsewhere only about a tenth as many droplets. Thin spray means fewer droplets, not smaller ones (each
  droplet in the splatter picture now has its own rank). The fans wash off like the rest of the mud.
- **Seen**: from the side the droplets now sit in two patches, behind the rear wheel and behind the front wheel, with the
  middle of the doors nearly clean (26 droplets in a box behind the rear arch against 2 mid-door); the line reads as a slope
  rising to each arch, not a skyline. The droplets are round spots without streaks. The rear of the car is less coated than
  before (the line sits lower there). After the ford only a thin ragged strip of mud is left above the clean waterline.
- **Found on the way**: the test drive parked the car with the brake held, and holding the brake at a stop is reverse - so after

- **One clean set of pictures** with no game open: the carried tracks (faint in this run), the muddy grass feet (from a verge

# 2026-10-08 (later): muddy rut pools (MudRunner MR3)

The third MudRunner lesson, cut to the owner's scope: "just rut pools for now, we are looking at improving water simulation".
The ford's water is being reworked separately and wasn't touched.

- **Churned water turns brown**: when a wheel moves the mud under a rut pool, or a thrown blob lands in one, that patch of water
  turns an opaque muddy brown (the Standard bog's colour, lightened and softly mottled). Each little cell of the mud field now
  carries how churned its water is.
- **Froth**: while the churn is fresh, light-brown specks float on the brown water, in irregular clusters of different sizes
  (drawn from the same painterly splatter picture as the car's mud droplets).
- **It settles**: the brown spreads a little into the water next to it, never into dry mud, and clears by half every 45
  seconds. Once it has faded away the mud field stops working on it. The ruts' shape never changes because of it.
- **Seen** (God of War was running, so the drive was slow and the scripted pass took the pictures): just after the car spun
  its wheels in Deep, the pool is opaque brown with clusters of pale froth specks; the specks are crisp and flat, more like
  spilt paint than foam. A minute later the froth is gone and the pool is a little more see-through, but still brown, so the
  change is small (the clear rut water was brown already).
