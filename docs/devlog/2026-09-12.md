- Weather is one subsystem with five state assets and a storm pressure that fronts report every
  tick, so a front owns no weather of its own. Ground wetness and snow cover are the two slow
  quantities; the collection, the sun, the sky light and the fog follow. Rain is greybox
  instanced streaks with landing and cover traces (drops land on roofs and never enter the space
  under them; Niagara is an M6 line) plus a procedural ambience. The occlusion capture is an
  orthographic depth capture over 60 m that MF_Weather reads to keep wetness and snow off
  covered ground, with puddles from a value noise on pooling ground as the wetness rises. Wet
  traction: the car feeds the subsystem's wetness into the traction, and dirt gained wet rolling
  and sinkage scales so it drives like shallow mud in the rain.
- Conditions: three Tier 1 ones (the GDD is not written, so this is the proposal in code): heat
  as a coolant model, dust as a filter clog that only clears at the base, cold as a torque
  multiplier and an exposure drain. Each ramps performance -> warning -> damage with a lamp or
  dial and a HUD line at each stage, and only the last stage costs engine health, slowly. The
  environment stage sits between traction and condition in the tuning pipeline; parts and
  consumables will subtract from the intensities by tag in M4.
