# 2026-09-18

## W4's follow-up: refraction, and the shallows

The user, on foot at the ford after W4: "That looks better. but the water is still too transparent, or there is no
refraction so in the clear parts of the water you cant really tell it is there." Two causes named, and the day's
finding is that they do not carry equal weight.

Refraction went in properly. `MP_REFRACTION` is connected and the material's `refraction_method` is
`RM_PIXEL_NORMAL_OFFSET` - the mode for a flat sheet whose vertex normals are all up and whose whole shape lives in
the pixel normal - and it coexists with the per-pixel translucency lighting, so nothing was given up to get it. The pin
takes `lerp(1.0, RefractionIOR 1.33, RefractionStrength x an 8 cm edge fade x the dry mask)`, which makes 1.0 "no
bending" in either of 5.8's modes and `RefractionStrength` 0 a clean off switch; a new `gt.Water.Refraction` pushes it
onto every sheet's own instance, and a new picture pair uses it with the surface frozen - wind off, flow off, field
calmed - so the two frames differ by the refraction and nothing else.

They differ by almost nothing. `hide_30_ford_refract_on` against `_off` moved 0.00 % of its box by more than 12 of
255, a mean change of 1.15 and a biggest of 6, against a gate of 5 %. `RM_INDEX_OF_REFRACTION` was then built and
measured on the same pair: 0.02 %, mean 2.01. The mechanism is the same for both - the engine's distortion offset is
bounded by how far the refracting surface stands in front of what is behind it, and over a ford's bed that gap is the
depth of the water, 18 cm a metre past the waterline. The bend is a fraction of a pixel whatever the mode. Pixel
Normal Offset ships because it is the right mode for this surface and carries no shoreline halo, not because it
measured better.

What moved the shallows is the other half of what he said - too transparent. `ShallowOpacity` 0.25 to 0.45,
`WaterDeepCm` 120 to 70 (120 put the tint's ramp out past anything a ford holds), `FresnelOpacity` 0.30 to 0.45, and a
wet bed: over the first 5 cm of depth the tint is multiplied 0.35 darker and the opacity lifted by the same ramp, so
the waterline reads as a wet line on the bank. The lift is not decoration - a translucent surface cannot multiply what
is behind it, so darkening a tint at an opacity of 0.25 darkens nothing.

The gate this was all supposed to be judged on turned out to be broken, which is the day's other finding. hide_29's
colour distance between its shallow box and its "dry edge" box read 37.3, 25.8 and 6.4 across three runs of the same
material, the two means swapping which is brighter. The edge box is not dry ground at all - it is the water half a
metre to two metres past the waterline - so the number differences shallow water against slightly shallower water, and
the 9 it was being compared against was measured the same way. And the eye is placed against a waterline found by
walking the route, which the field puts at 1110.0, 1110.5 and 1111.5 m on three runs, so the boxes frame different
ground each time. Fixing it means the edge box on the bank before the waterline and the eye on a fixed arc; there was
no build left for it, and nothing about the water was tuned to chase it. hide_30's own box, frozen by construction,
read 129 136 128 in all three runs to within a unit, and is the instrument the next pass should use.

The standing gates hold: the motion pair 26.38 % flowing against 0.00 % still, hide_23's water box 7.10 % bright
against a gate of 25, hide_22's water 155 168 161 at 79.5 % blue over red. hide_23's bow box fell from 14.68 % to
0.39 % over 200, reported and not gated - a darker, less blown-out bow wave is what was asked for. `water` PASSES all
ten of its gates, one build, exit 0, and only the water materials were rebuilt.

Not driven, not felt, and nobody has looked at a picture.

## Reverted the same day

The user drove the follow-up: "Revert it, that seems to have messed up the physics of the water." All of it is out.
`M_GT_Water` is back to W4's shipped 39 expressions - ShallowOpacity 0.25, WaterDeepCm 120, FresnelOpacity 0.30, no
MP_REFRACTION connection, no refraction method, no wet bed - the `gt.Water.Refraction` cvar and the sheet's
`AppliedRefraction` are gone, the hide_30 pair is out of the runner and the sampler, and the ford shots write eleven
pictures again. `CalmField`, `CalmAll` and `FGTWaterField::Calm` were kept: they pre-date the follow-up, the water
test uses them in four places and shots step 26 in one, and hide_30 was only another caller. The refraction finding
stays in section 45 because it is the reason it does not come back: both of 5.8's modes measured 0.00-0.02 % on a
frozen on/off pair, the distortion offset being bounded by the 18 cm between the surface and the bed.

Why it broke the water is not proven and no instrumentation was built to chase it before the revert. The follow-up
touched no solver, hull, traction or subsystem file. The suspect is frame time: the field takes at most four
sub-steps a frame, and a distortion pass over a 35,520-vertex translucent sheet lengthens frames. The rendered shots
run logs no clamp count and no StepMs - the water test logs both, but it runs under NullRHI, where there is no
distortion pass to see, so that test could never have caught this. The one proxy the logs carry is game seconds per
real second over shots steps 22-28, and it is bimodal and does not separate the two builds: W4-main read 0.996 and
0.272 on two runs of the same build, the three follow-up runs 0.420, 0.375 and 0.249, and the reverted build 0.996.
What that does say is that the ford pictures are frame-rate dependent - at 0.27 the field gets a quarter of the
sub-steps per game second it gets at 1.0 - which is the likeliest reason two runs of the same build disagree by more
than the follow-up ever moved them, and it is worth measuring before anyone trusts a ford picture to a few per cent.

The picture numbers, W4-main against after the revert: hide_22's water box 166 175 163 against 171 179 166, blue over
red 66.1 against 34.7 %, over 200 8.41 against 4.44 %; hide_23's water 8.53 against 5.40 % bright and its bow box
14.68 against 14.92 %; the motion pair 34.34 against 13.42 % flowing, both 0.00 % still, both passing their gates;
hide_24 31.98 against 7.34 %. `water` PASSES all ten gates and `terrain` fails step 11's held exit alone, the standing
flake, with all six ford gates 1. Two builds, both exit 0, and only the water materials were rebuilt.
