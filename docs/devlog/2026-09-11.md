# 2026-09-11 – First car blockout into Unreal, and it drives

- Hand-modelled Range Rover Classic blockout finished in `Art/rangerover.blend` (18 objects,
  ~4.4k tris). Measured against the 108-inch LSE it is within 3 % on length, width, height and
  wheelbase. The scripted chassis/jig from 09-08/09 is gone; the body is now the source of truth.
- Export pipeline: `Art/export_rangerover.bat` (headless Blender, never touches the .blend)
  writes `Art/Export/SK_RangeRover.fbx` (Root + four wheel bones at the hubs), one static mesh
  per panel with mirrored pairs split L/R, and `manifest.json`. Details and the object naming
  rules in VEHICLE_BUILD_SYSTEM.md section 12.
- Exporter traps fixed: scene unit scale must be 0.01 when the data is already in centimetres
  (else 100x too big in Unreal); the armature must be named `Armature` with meshes unparented or
  Unreal keeps it as an extra root bone; bones must point along the car's left so the root bone
  imports with identity rotation, otherwise Chaos gets the vehicle frame wrong.
- Import pipeline: `Art/import_rangerover.bat` runs the editor headless with the Python plugin
  enabled on the command line and imports into `Content/GrandTour/Vehicles/Bodies/RangeRover`.
  Verified: 461 x 186 x 179 cm, front +X, left -Y, bones at the hubs, scale 1.
- Enabled ChaosVehiclesPlugin in the .uproject and added `ChaosVehicles` to Build.cs.
- M0 vehicle in C++: `AGTCarPawn`, `UGTVehicleWheelFront/Rear`, `UGTVehicleAnimInstance` (wheel
  bones posed in code, no Animation Blueprint), `AGTDriveTestGameMode`, plus
  `UGTVehicleSetupLibrary` for scripted setup. `Art/setup_m0.bat` makes the physics asset (one
  box, no wheel bodies) and the flat test map `L_DriveTest`; the project now opens on it.
- `Art/drive_test.bat` proves it headless: PASS, 0 to 73 km/h in 7 s, 1-2 shift at 3950 rpm,
  straight line, 79.6 m in 8 s. M0's "a car drives on a flat test map" is met.
- Not done from the M0 row: Git + LFS (the repo is not initialised). Next: open the editor, drive
  it by hand, check the camera and input signs, then materials and the M1 greybox track.

## Later the same day – M1 slice 1: surfaces, tuning pipeline, condition

- `UGTSurfacePhysMaterial` (dry grip in Friction, wet grip, rolling drag, sinkage, track depth,
  surface tag) with PM_Tarmac / Dirt / Mud / Snow; native tags `Surface.*` in GTGameplayTags.
- The tuning pipeline from ARCHITECTURE 6 exists: `UGTVehicleTuningComponent` rebuilds
  `FGTVehicleTuning` every tick as base -> `UGTTractionComponent` -> `UGTConditionComponent`
  and writes changed values to Chaos (max torque, drag, per-wheel friction). Rolling resistance
  is a body force, not a Chaos parameter.
- `UGTTractionComponent`: per-wheel sinkage that grows on soft ground (faster when slow or
  spinning), recovers on firm ground, feeds grip loss and drag, bogged flag.
- `UGTConditionComponent`: 14 parts with health, fuel with idle + load burn (x10 time
  compression) and tank leaks, misfires and torque loss from engine health, grip loss from tyre
  health, steering pull from uneven suspension damage, radiator damage cooking the engine, 32-slot
  merged dent list. Body hit events resolve to damage regions in car space.
- Seat camera: chassis lag, stable-horizon roll/pitch removal, free look with auto-recentre.
- `Art/setup_m1_track.bat` builds `L_TestTrack` (600 x 200 m tarmac floor, four 60 m surface
  lanes ending in a wall, and a climb, plateau, descent, jump, rough patch and slalom at
  y = +40 m) plus the environment master material `M_GT_Environment` and six instances. Project
  now opens on L_TestTrack.
- `Art/surface_test.bat` headless proof: PASS. Full throttle: tarmac 0 -> 74 km/h, dirt sinkage
  rises, mud slows 83 -> 78 km/h with grip 0.87, snow to 74 km/h with grip 0.76, wall hit at
  74 km/h registers a front impact (deltaV 2580 cm/s): radiator 0, engine 0.37, torque x0.64,
  0.9 L fuel burned. Skeletal mesh hit events needed the notify flag on the physics bodies.
- Next in M1: displacement spike, track paint, cockpit mesh + dashboard component, impact
  texture into the body material, panel detach, dirt/wet body state, spray/audio, fun test.
- First hand drive feedback: mud too sticky from a stop, rough patch very jolty. Fixes: sinkage
  now grows mostly from rolling and wheelspin (sitting still is 30 % of the rate), mud and snow
  drag reduced and a speed-squared ploughing term added so speed is what mud punishes; bogging
  needs wheelspin and clears when you ease off. Wheels sweep a sphere, suspension smoothing 4,
  more travel; eye point damped vertically; ridges replace the boxes. New headless tests:
  `Art/mud_test.bat` (PASS: parked 4 s, then 0 to 44 km/h) and `Art/rough_test.bat`. Surface and
  flat regressions still pass. Trap: Python `unreal.Rotator` is (roll, pitch, yaw).
- Greybox cockpit: `UGTDashboardComponent` builds a dash slab, speedo, tacho and fuel dials with
  lagged needles, five warning lights (engine, coolant, fuel, bogged, suspension) and a steering
  wheel that turns with the input, all from engine primitives with dynamic instances of the new
  `M_GT_Prop` master (`Art/setup_m1_cockpit.bat`). Found on the way: a controller left plugged in
  fed stick drift into the Steer action and once slammed it to full lock, so gamepad axes now
  have a 0.2 dead zone and the headless tests disable player input on the car.
- Cockpit fixes after the second hand drive: the steering wheel was yawing about the car's
  vertical axis (rotator yaw is parent-relative) and was a solid disc that hid the dials. Now a
  tilted pivot with a spinning child and a ring of twelve rim segments, the cluster raised to
  Z 121 above the rim, and a binnacle hood behind it. The surface test now also traces eye to
  dial against every greybox part's bounds and reports "clear".
- Third hand drive: cockpit "looks much better". Fixed the steering wheel snapping to the key
  direction (it now follows the front wheels' rate-limited steer angle) and stiffened the
  suspension (spring 400, preload 60, damping 0.75, smoothing 2) for "soft and floaty". Flat and
  rough regressions pass. Session ended here; next slice is the tyre-track render target.

## Later still – suspension retune and tyre tracks

- Feel check on the last two changes: the steering wheel visual "looks right"; the stiffened
  suspension (400 / 0.75) is "slightly too jolty". Read the Chaos 5.8 source before touching it:
  spring force is compression from full drop times rate, damping is ratio times critical,
  SpringPreload is never used and SuspensionSmoothing only smooths the drawn wheel. Set 340 /
  0.62 (1.28 Hz, halfway between floaty and jolty). The rough test now also reports RMS g, the
  single-step vertical velocity jump, and settle time / bounces after the last ridge; the 50 ms
  peak g turned out to swing 3.4..6.3 between identical runs, while the step jump is stable: 18
  cm/s before, 12..14 now, settling in 0.1 s with no bounce. Not yet driven by the user.
- Tyre tracks (ARCHITECTURE 6a): `UGTTrackPaintSubsystem`, a 128 m / 2048 px render target
  that follows the car, wheel streaks added per tick with a frame-rate independent deposit,
  whole-texel scrolling on recentre, a slow multiplicative fade, a CPU grid mirror for gameplay
  and tests, and `MF_TrackPaint` (one custom HLSL node) feeding Normal and World Position
  Offset of `M_GT_Environment` behind a per-instance TrackStrength. `Art/setup_m1_trackpaint.bat`
  builds the assets; `Art/track_test.bat` passes headless and with `gpu` (off-screen D3D12,
  render target read back agrees with the grid). Details and traps in VEHICLE_BUILD_SYSTEM.md
  section 13.
- Body panels, dents and detach (ARCHITECTURE 6b): `UGTBodyComponent` attaches the 20 imported
  panel meshes at the car origin and hides the skeletal body, so the panels are the body; the
  condition's impact list becomes a 32 x 2 float texture read by `MF_Dents` in the new
  `M_GT_Vehicle` (inward push plus analytic normal, per vertex and pixel, across panel seams);
  each damage region has its own health and at zero its panels come off as physics props; a
  greybox windscreen carries `M_GT_Glass` with a Crack parameter. `Art/setup_m1_body.bat`
  builds the materials, `Art/body_test.bat` (and `gpu`) drives into the wall and checks it all.
  Found on the way: a frontal wall hit resolved to the roof because the box contact point sat
  on the top edge (regions now go by hit direction first); freshly detached panels overlapped
  the chassis box and the solver launched the car 150 m up (they ignore the car now); a material
  loaded by a constructor finder is rooted and the setup script cannot rebuild it (load in
  BeginPlay). `-GTProbeMaterial=` on an off-screen run is the only way to see custom-HLSL compile
  errors; commandlets stay silent, proved with a deliberately broken node.
- Body surface state (ARCHITECTURE 6b/6d): `UGTBodyStateComponent` accumulates dirt per region
  from wheel spray by surface tag, wetness that dries and snow that melts; every panel now has
  its own material instance carrying Dirt/Wetness/Snow; `M_GT_Vehicle` grimes from the sills up
  and ends in the new `MF_Weather` (reads `MPC_Weather`), which is the function every master
  will go through. `Art/setup_m1_weather.bat` + `Art/setup_m1_body.bat` build it,
  `Art/bodystate_test.bat` (and `gpu`) verify it. First run showed sides as dirty as arches
  (two wheels feed a side) and wetness saturating in two seconds (four wheels summed): sides
  now take 0.35 per wheel and wet/snow follow the strongest wheel.
- Spray and audio (ARCHITECTURE 6a), greybox because neither Niagara nor sound assets can be
  authored headless: `UGTSprayComponent` emits instanced-sphere mud, dust and snow per wheel by
  surface tag and speed; `UGTVehicleAudioComponent` synthesises engine, tyre (low-passed by
  surface), skid and impact-thud audio through a procedural sound wave. All headless tests now
  run with `-nosound`; `Art/spray_test.bat` checks spray by lane and renders a synth block to
  check the mix has signal (PASS). Trap of the day: a failed build followed by a test run left
  the old binary in the game with an unknown test flag and it never exited.
- Master materials: `Art/setup_m1_masters.bat` creates M_GT_Foliage (wind sway from
  MPC_Weather) and M_GT_Character, routes the environment, prop and glass masters through
  `MF_Weather` behind a per-master Porosity, and asserts all six masters end in it. An
  off-screen run with the new masters on probe cubes compiled everything. That closes the M1
