- **The Proving Ground (L_Proving)**, a compact map to feel water, mud and the car on without driving a leg
  (VEHICLE_BUILD_SYSTEM 47). A 378 m landscape (3 x 3 components, the fewest that hold it) with a 697 m loop off a
  60 x 60 m tarmac skid pan at the middle: ford A 0.6 m with 14 rocks, ford B 1.4 m with four winch posts, a 60 m bog
  with four, a 120 m rally stretch (crest, compression, banked corner), an 80 m track, three 30 m ramps at 15 / 25 /
  35 % with flat tops and a 40 m traverse across 18 degrees. New in the generator beside the old: `FGTTerrainPlane`
  (rectangles laid over the land after the roads: the pad, the ramps, the slope), `FGTTerrainStation` and
  `FGTRouteSection::FordRockCountOverride`; every leg spec leaves them empty. `setup_m6_hide.py`'s build path is now
  parameterised and `setup_m6_proving.py` drives it, so one code path builds both maps - The Hide rebuilt unchanged
  through it. `GTGoto <station>` on the player controller (pad forda fordb bog rally track climbs climbs25 climbs35
  slope) lands the car 10 m before a station at rest; the names and the water / weather cvars go on screen for 15 s at
  BeginPlay on a map with stations. `Art/play_proving.bat` launches it windowed. New test `proving` (24th in the
  suite): PASS in 38 s, every count as the spec asks (sheets 2, patch 1, rocks 14, stones 20, rally posts 6, winch
  posts 8), every station landed at rest with its own reading (fords 61.4 and 139.9 cm deep, grades 15.0 / 25.0 /
  35.0 %, cross slope 18.0). `terrain` 279 s and `water` 304 s on The Hide PASS after the change. Minimum gap between
  footprints 13.0 m. The shallow-mud station was skipped: `BogCoreFraction` is the spec's, not a section's. Nobody has
  driven it.
