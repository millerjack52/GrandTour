# Benmore distant hill detail

The previous backdrop used 1,009 samples along each inner edge but only 51 radial rows across the surrounding region. In the widest patch, outer rows were about 230 m apart. This produced long thin triangles, smoothed away real drainage folds and left visibly faceted silhouettes. The material also faded close rock normals away by 350 m, leaving the distant geometry to carry all lighting detail. Maximum fog opacity of 0.72 further obscured that detail.

## Implemented treatment

- Four new scenery meshes sample the same LINZ elevation source on a regular 20 m grid: 846,328 source triangles combined, rendered with Nanite. The old four mesh assets remain available. Geographic extent, heights and vertical scale are retained. The inner 5 m overlap and 35 m transition use the existing canonical playable terrain. Background actors have no collision.
- A 10 m world-normal texture reconstructs the real surface orientation between background mesh vertices. The playable landscape keeps its finer original geometry and bank normals: replacing those with the regional normal map proved too smooth in visual review. The backdrop uses the regional normal directly, preserving topographic shading beyond the previous close-detail fade.
- A second geographic texture contains smoothed downhill accumulation, 320 m ridge/gully position, slope, and 80 m local convexity. These fields place subdued dark vegetation in drainage channels, lighter mineral scree on suitable slopes, and broken grey rock on steep exposed areas. The distant colour treatment blends in over 80–350 m and preserves road masks.
- Maximum height-fog opacity is reduced from 0.72 to 0.52. Weather still controls fog density; the existing blue-grey colour and 280 m start remain.

The flow mask is an artistic drainage indicator, not a hydrological simulation: local pits remain sinks and it creates no water. The normal map and masks derive from the downloaded terrain; the soil/rock colours are an art-directed interpretation of the user's reference photos, not local scanned material classifications.

## Rebuild and restore

1. Terrain Python: `Art/tools/prepare_benmore_hills.py`.
2. Blender: `Art/Blender/export_benmore_hills.py`.
3. Unreal: `Art/Unreal/setup_benmore_hills.py`.
4. Unreal: `Art/Unreal/refine_benmore_hills.py` updates only materials when tuning the look.
5. Unreal: `Art/Unreal/check_benmore_hills.py`.

Outputs and before/after images are in `Art/Export/BenmoreHills`. The setup copies the map to `L_Benmore_BeforeHills` and the previous two terrain materials to `Hills/M_BeforeHills_*`. The previous shader source is `before_benmore_bank_surface.py`. To restore, use the old map plus copied materials/source; a map backup alone does not undo shared material edits.

Playable heights, road geometry and collision, rocks, trees, tussocks, scrub and fences are not regenerated by this pass. Other contributors' assets and shared C++ are not changed.

## Validation

Validated 2026-10-07. `setup-report.json`, `refinement-report.json` and the final `final-visual-report.json` all pass.

- All 644 sampled source vertices match after FBX import within 0.125 cm. Four saved scenery actors have zero translation, Nanite enabled and no collision. Authoring-grid cell dimensions are at most 20 m on both axes.
- The two geographic textures retain their 1368 x 1277 resolution, linear colour and uncompressed vector-data settings. Terrain/backdrop material compilation passes.
- The canonical playable height hash still matches the previous pass. All 57,808 road/verge instances, 923 scrub instances and 348 fence parts are retained. The settled car has four wheel contacts; all 21 road-centre clearance traces pass.
- Visual review caught an overly smooth regional-normal replacement on the playable hills. The final shader retains their original geometry/bank normals, lowers scree slope thresholds to suit the terrain and blends the geographic colours more gently. The background retains its new geometry and 10 m normal map.
- Final images are `Hillside_Vista.png` and `Sheltered_Scrub.png`, captured at the same camera locations as their `Before_*` counterparts with normal `gt.Look.Post 2` rendering and a 100-second grass warm-up. `Initial_Terrace_Fence.png` records the earlier material iteration, not the final material.

Unattended editor timings are not a performance benchmark. The six driving scenarios from the earlier environment pass were not rerun; this pass does not modify playable collision or road heights.
