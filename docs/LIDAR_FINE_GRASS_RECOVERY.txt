LiDAR fine grass restoration / 19 November 2026
==============================================
Status: v2 installed in the normal LiDAR map, saved/reloaded and visually checked.

the earlier fine LiDAR grass, not the grass in FloraTrail.
The previous forest-path review did not adequately cover the open paddocks.

Identified causes
The 13 November FloraWorld installer assigned Flora Paint Grass_005/013/016
and Dry_Grass_014 to the native streamed grass actor. Its modified plant pigment
graph creates broad green and rusty/brown clumps. The distant layer still uses
the older GS3 geometry, while terrain/far colour uses the separate RS1 palette.
The 19 November crown/grade transfer preserved those bindings; it did not create
the broad-clump replacement. Existing good grass art remains under GrassGS3.

Controlled correction
Restore SM_GS3_Near_A/B/C and SM_GS3_Verge with their original wind/contact
materials copied into map-local GrassRecovery/v2 assets. Keep GS3 far geometry.
Change only the existing shared pasture palette constants across near grass,
far grass, verge, terrain and detail material copies. No global desaturation,
emissive fill, new texture samples, lighting adjustment or tree recolour.
Three palette roles remain: living green, cool green and restricted dry straw.
The first muted v1 was too grey-straw; it is retained as diagnostic evidence.
v2 restores more green at similar restrained brightness.

Geometry and budget
The earlier fine assets have 144/64/36 triangles per near clump and 216/96/60
per verge clump; far remains 72/12. They are not free compared with the replaced
38-42 triangle Flora clumps. Existing spacing (55cm), scales (.9-1.15), streaming
radius (46m), near fade (32-40m), LOD distances (7/16m), far spacing (2m) and far
end (150m) remain fixed. Retaining those limits does not prove equal GPU cost.
The same native host, planting rules, custom-data contact contract and shaders
for wind/removal are retained. Physical terrain, soil, mud and water are protected.
The terrain/detail colour edit remains inside the existing pasture mask; native
displacement, opacity, wet-mud graph and texture bindings are unchanged.

Review
Compare current, fine-original and fine-muted-v1 in the actual LiDAR map using
two open-paddock cameras and the previous mixed-material bank camera. Current
captures are 1274x682; subsequent PIE windows in that same process expanded to
1600x868. That intermediate three-way diagnosis is NOT an identical-resolution
comparison. The final saved candidate is checked against the initial current
captures at native 1274x682, using the same cameras/FOV/quality/exposure settings.
No source PNG is recoloured or enhanced. Auto exposure and wind time can vary;
these are not pixel-identical simulation snapshots or real-time benchmarks.
Grass streaming completes before capture; native contact API probes check bending
and removal. They do not substitute for a new physical driving/reset test.

Tree assessment
The tree materials, LODs, placement, lighting and grade are held fixed here.
Broad mustard/olive lit masses, dark interiors/trunks and repeated silhouettes
remain. The previous bright field amplifies that mismatch, but correcting grass
does not solve canopy colour/form. Next tree tests should isolate leaf pigment
from normal response under open-edge and back/side light; keep selected warm
highlights and readable green interiors instead of darkening/desaturating all trees.

Workflow and recovery
Art/run_lidar_grass_recovery.ps1: audit, prepare, compare (v1 diagnostic),
candidate (current configured palette), install (save, reload, gameplay checks).
Art/lidar_grass_recovery_settings.json records the selected palette.
Art/Export/LidarGrassRecovery contains source reports, captures and owner feedback.
Current C: backup is created by Art/lidar_grass_recovery_baseline.py only after
a completed comparison; it protects the post-tree-transfer map independently
of the older pre-transfer backup. Historical D: is still unavailable.
Other original LiDAR packages and FloraTrail remain protected by hashes.
The previous transfer's exact map hash becomes historical after this correction.
Do not run the old tree-transfer rollback over this later map; its guard refuses it.
A deliberate full FloraWorld regeneration still restores its older broad-grass
bindings. That workflow must preserve the focused recovery.apply mapping and
establish a fresh baseline; do not blindly replay this one-time guarded install
against later edits. Do not regenerate the full forest just to change grass.

Recovery command (renderer closed):
powershell -NoProfile -File Art/rollback_lidar_grass_recovery.ps1
This restores the pre-grass map, retaining the earlier tree transfer. It verifies
the backup and refuses to overwrite a map changed after this installation.
