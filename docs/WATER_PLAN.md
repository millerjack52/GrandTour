# Water, the MudRunner way: a current, a car that floats, one water model, the look, rivers that rise (plan for implementing agents)

Written 2026-09-16 after the mud was parked at stage J of `docs/MUD_ONE_SURFACE_PLAN.md` ("good enough for now").
Five stages, W1 to W5, one Opus session each, every stage gated by the user driving it (water physics is movement)
and, where the look changes, by a picture. Written so an agent can start from this file and the read list below
without the conversation that produced it.

## 1. What is there now (2026-09-16)

- **A ford is a sheet.** `AGTWaterSheet` (Terrain/): an `AStaticMeshActor` with the engine cube, no collision,
  movable, one per `Ford` section, spawned by `setup_m6_hide.py` at the section's middle scaled to (length + 24 m,
  30 m across the route, 0.1) at `lib.terrain_ford_water_level_cm` (the bed's lowest point + `DepthM` 0.6). Level =
  `BaseLevelCm` + `RiseWithWetnessCm` 80 x the weather's ground wetness + `ExtraRiseCm` (tests, events); it moves
  itself each tick. `DepthCmAt(World, GroundPoint)` is a static query over every sheet (0 outside, 0 where the ground
  is above the level); `SetExtraRiseAll` raises them all; `Covers(XY)`.
- **What the car reads.** Per wheel `UGTTractionComponent` takes `WaterDepthM` at the contact: drag `WaterDragPerMetre`
  2.4 of the load per metre of depth at 10 m/s with speed squared (inside the 0.9 drag cap shared with mud), and a
  grip loss `WaterBuoyancyGripLoss` 0.4 at `WaterBuoyancyDepthM` 0.8 (a stand-in: the car does not lighten).
  `FrontWaterDepthM` goes to `UGTConditionComponent::UpdateFlooding`: past `IntakeHeightM` 0.85 the engine floods
  (`bFlooded`: no torque, the lamp, the audio off) until `FloodDrainSeconds` 5 out of it. The water does not push,
  float or sweep the car.
- **The rut water is separate.** A Mud section's `WaterTableCm` -25 (relative to the undisturbed ground) plus
  `AGTMudPatch::WetTableLiftCm` 15 x wetness; `AGTMudPatch::WaterDepthCmAt` reads it per cell. **Since W3 it is not
  separate**: the patches register with `UGTWaterSubsystem` and `Sample` answers their water as kind `Rut` (the deeper
  of sheet and patch, never the sum), the traction's own max-of-two branch is gone and
  `UGTMudSubsystem::bRutWaterPhysics` was **removed**, so the rut water counts. The water grid wears `M_GT_MudWater`
  (setup_m6_water.py, brown, depth-faded).
- **The look.** `M_GT_Water` from `build_water_material` in setup_m6_hide.py: translucent, two-sided, BaseColor 0.08
  0.18 0.22, Roughness 0.05, Opacity 0.65, no texture, no normal, no motion, its own master outside `MF_Weather`;
  built once (`if exists: return`), so a change needs the asset rebuilt.
- **The tests.** Terrain step 13: the ford at 20 km/h in low range with the lock, through in 0.60 m with the engine
  running; step 14: `SetExtraRiseAll` 70 cm, the engine floods in 1.30 m and the car does not get through (the result
  line `ford [...]`). No picture of a ford in `hide_shots`; `Art/bog_shots.bat` is the fast-picture pattern.
- **Around it.** The weather: `GetWetness` (dries at 1 %/s), `GetRainIntensity`, `GetRainAt`, `SetWetnessForTest`;
  fronts in `Weather/GTWeatherFront`. The winch: `UGTWinchComponent` + `AGTWinchAnchor` (steel posts 200 kN; one
  `Post_Ford` 12 m beside the route at 1150 m, by the ford's exit). The spray: `UGTSprayComponent` (the `spray` test).
  The car: a Chaos wheeled vehicle, CoM override (22.6, 0, 60) cm, the chassis body never sleeps; **its mass is not in
  this plan** (read it at BeginPlay from the mesh's body instance; every buoyancy number below is a fraction of it).

## 2. The target (MudRunner / SnowRunner)

A river is a thing that moves. The current is visible in the water and pushes the truck downstream; the deeper the
water the harder it pushes, and in deep water the truck is carried, loses the road and ends against the bank or in a
hole. Past the intake the engine dies and only the winch gets you out. The truck does not sail, but it does go
light: wheels lose their bite, the body drifts, and here the user wants that taken one step further, a real float
past a depth. Rivers rise after rain, so the crossing you made on the way out can be closed on the way back. At the
banks the water foams; the truck pushes a bow wave and the wheels throw water.

**Revised 2026-09-16 after W2 (the user: "what I want is the water to move around like water, like waves").** A plane
with forces on the car is not that. The water surface itself is simulated: a shallow-water height field per ford
(the mud field's pattern: a CPU grid, a mesh over it, the physics reading it), so the current is a real flow across
the bed, a disturbance spreads as rings and reflects off the banks, the car pushes a bow wave and drags a wake, wind
and rain stir the surface, and the car floats on the local surface rather than on a level. Stages WS1 and WS2 do
that and go before W3; W1 and W2's forces carry over unchanged, reading the field instead of the plane.

## 3. Stages

Order: **W1 -> W2 (built) -> WS1 -> WS2 -> W3 -> W5 -> W4**. W1 birthed the one water query
(`UGTWaterSubsystem::Sample`); WS1 puts a simulated surface under it and WS2 gives that surface its sources (the
car, wind, rain); W3 then folds the rut water in; W5 drives the field's boundary head with the rain; W4 is the
texture, foam and spray on top of the simulated surface. Strictly serial: one Unreal process, one agent at a time.
W2's drive gate can wait until WS1: the sim changes what the car reads, the lift and drag numbers do not change.

- **The spec.** `FGTRouteSection` gains `FlowSpeedMps` (Ford only, default **2.0**) and `FlowYawOffsetDeg` (default
  **90**: the flow runs across the route, from the car's left to its right at the section's mid heading; Unreal's yaw
  is clockwise from above, so heading + 90 is the car's right). A field never edited in `DA_Terrain_Hide` follows the

  hands both to the sheet at spawn (as it hands `base_level_cm`).
- **The sheet.** `AGTWaterSheet` gains `FlowDirection` (unit, world XY), `FlowSpeedCmPerS`, `FlowFadeM` **5** (the
  flow fades to zero over the last 5 m at the sheet's two ends along the flow, so a carried car strands in still
  water instead of leaving the sheet and dropping onto the bed; the sheet's water ends at its edge) and
  `FlowAt(XY)`. `cvar gt.Water.FlowScale` (default 1, 0 = still water) and `gt.Water.ExtraRiseCm` (default 0; the

- **The one query.** New `UGTWaterSubsystem` (a world subsystem, Terrain/): sheets register at BeginPlay;

- **The hull.** New `UGTHullComponent` on the car pawn (the sibling of the traction): the car in water as a box.

  is capped at `MaxWaterForceG` **1.5** x the weight (the sim guard, as the drag cap is for mud). Applied through the
  same path the traction uses for its forces (read it; no second way of pushing the chassis).
- **The wheels.** `WaterDragPerMetre` is unchanged but its speed becomes the relative speed |v_wheel - v_flow| and

  lateral part is a force at the contact point on the chassis. A parked car in a current is pushed through its tyres
  as well as its body.
- **The tell.** `M_GT_Water` moves its builder into `Art/Unreal/setup_m6_water.py` (which already builds

### W2. Buoyancy: the car lightens, floats and is carried; the winch gets it back

*Built 2026-09-16 (VEHICLE_BUILD_SYSTEM 40, devlog 2026-09-16); not driven by the user. The plan's lift is in, on W1's
five samples and W1's force path, with the mass read at BeginPlay from the body instance: **2100 kg**, **20.6 kN**.
Two numbers are not the plan's. `HullVolumeM3` is **3.4**, not 3.0, because the raised ford stands 1.16 m over the keel
where the test parks the car and a 2100 kg car needs **3.13 m3** to float in that at all: 3.0 would have left it on the
bed at 0.97 of neutral, and that is the one state whose wheel load cannot be measured, the traction's load correction
(`Weight / SpringSum` clamped 0.5 to 5) reading a light spring back up to the full weight until the springs fall under a
fifth of it. 3.4 puts the neutral keel line at **1.05 m**, nine centimetres clear. And a number the plan did not have:
`LiftDampingRatio` **0.5** of critical (**6.4 kN per m/s**, shared out by each sample's submerged fraction), because
W1's drag zeroes its vertical part and the lift on its own is a 19.6 kN/m spring on 2100 kg - a **2.1 s bob that never
