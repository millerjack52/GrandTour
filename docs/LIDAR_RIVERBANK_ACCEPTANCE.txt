CORRECTION TO THE RECOVERY PLAN
Variation means the variety observed in these photographs, including variation
within fern forms. Do not impose equal proportions of arbitrary species, convert
the fern-rich interior into a shrub garden, or blanket the bank with clones.
The rocky-bank choice is localized exposed structure within this photographed
habitat; it does not authorize a continuous new cliff or a row of large boulders.

EVIDENCE PACK FOR EACH BUILD
For each view store: source photograph ID, relevant v03 panel, unedited baseline
game capture, candidate game capture, camera transform/FOV, map and asset revision,
lighting/exposure settings, actual renderer preset, date and verdict with reasons.
Use the actual in-game camera for baseline/candidate comparisons. The source photo
is a visual relationship reference; do not claim pixel registration or surveyed
camera position when these are unknown. Do not use image similarity scores as an
art-quality verdict across photographs, concepts and stylized game renders.

Fixed test views:
B01 - Across-river composition at standing eye height.
B02 - Oblique root/crown close view, approximately 2-5 m where safely reachable.
B03 - Along-bank view revealing overlaps, back faces and ledge intersections.
B04 - Normal vehicle approach and crossing view.
B05 - Shaded bank pocket with sunlit foreground in the same frame.
Distances are test-design targets, not surveyed values; save exact poses once set.
Record a repeatable walking/drive path through these views, including near/far
transitions, a direction reversal, and a stopped interval for temporal settling.

GATE A - LANDFORM AND COMPOSITION
Method: neutral-material blockout plus normal lit views B01/B03/B04.
Pass: continuous bank mass, subordinate exposed rock, credible river/plant/tree
scale, overlapping depth and intentionally open patches consistent with photos.
Block: repeated detached rock silhouettes, equally spaced shelves, oversized
boulders, loss of gully character, or a composition that works only from B01.
Evidence: matched stills from all three views. Geometry is revised before detail
if this gate fails.

GATE B - PLANT FORM, VARIETY AND SUPPORT
Method: selected plant turnarounds, LOD comparisons and views B01/B02/B03.
Pass: source-supported differences in crown height, frond width, division, age,
orientation and occlusion are visible at gameplay resolution. Upright centres and
hanging outer fronds remain volumetric. Soil/root pockets explain attachments.
Block: dominant repeated crown silhouettes, radial discs, potted shrubs, visible
root gaps, severe uphill frond clipping, or collapsed shape at cross-river distance.
Technical checks: actual supporting surface and root envelope; representative
frond clearance along the uphill/outward directions; stable roots during wind;
saved/reloaded placement equality. Use a small centimetre-scale root tolerance
matched to the actual crown rather than burying every asset by one universal value.
Contacts passing numerically cannot override a visible failure.

GATE C - MATERIALS AND LIGHT
Method: base-colour, normal and lighting diagnostics plus B02/B05 in final shading.
Pass: rock, gravel, soil, moss, bark and leaves have compatible detail scales;
shade retains material/colour distinctions; dampness follows believable contact
and moisture zones; sunlit foliage retains shape.
Block: unrelated texture grain, white-bark substitution, neon moss caps, plastic
leaves, crushed bank shadows, blanket glow, or obvious pasted-on material seams.
Compare under the chosen fixed exposure for diagnosis, then under normal gameplay
exposure. No edited screenshots or temporary lighting rigs count as final evidence.

GATE D - MOVEMENT AND GAMEPLAY
Method: native saved gameplay preset and the repeatable route in standalone/PIE.
Pass: crowns keep their shape across actual LOD transitions; no distracting
flicker/ghosting, root swimming, disappearing groundcover or broken water edges.
Crossings and road clearance remain usable; visible obstacles agree with collision.
Block: success only in a still image or only with forced highest-detail meshes;
new phantom collision, water/ground mismatch or changed mud/ford behaviour.
Temporary LOD0/native-resolution diagnostic settings must be removed before passing.

GATE E - PERFORMANCE AND REPRODUCIBILITY
Target: existing RTX4050 Laptop 6GB, 1600x900, saved v6 visual preset.
Run the same warmed route before and after on the same hardware/settings, with
repeat runs to distinguish variation. Record GPU and frame timings, p95, hitch
counts and VRAM. Preserve raw measurements and report uncertainty.
The recovery plan's roughly 1 ms additional GPU budget is provisional, not a
measured allowance or 120 fps promise. Any overrun needs explicit review and a
bounded optimization preserving the approved silhouettes and visual preset.
Check deterministic rebuild, no cumulative scaling/tilting/removal, outside-region
preservation, paired tree visuals/colliders, backup, rollback and save/reload.

RELEASE RULE
The complete representative section is reviewed first. Every gate has one of:
NOT TESTED / FAIL / PASS, an evidence link and a short reason. A missing view or
unrun test stays NOT TESTED. Do not average a critical failure into an overall pass.
User visual acceptance is recorded separately from automated technical results.
Only expand the treatment once the section passes the technical gates and its
visual direction is accepted. The earlier requirement to keep the live level
untouched applied to reference preparation. The owner's subsequent instruction
to build the first section authorized the bounded RS7 implementation.
