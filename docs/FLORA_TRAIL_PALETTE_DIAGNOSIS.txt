FloraTrail: first controlled Unreal palette diagnosis / 14 November 2026
======================================================================

Result
The diagnostic run completed successfully: 18 native 1274 x 682 game captures,
exit code zero, no shader-error fallback, 4K source bark and three foliage LODs
verified, 784 protected files unchanged. Standard midgrey output was RGB
(118,118,118.28), expected 117.65. This is a midgrey transfer check, not a full
display-curve characterization. No candidate was installed or accepted.

Appearance findings from actual road/lower-canopy images
* Identity probe retains the baseline's overall colour and form. Fine temporal
  coverage pixels differ; sample crown-crop mean absolute channel differences
  range about 2.1-4.4 on a 0-255 scale. This is not a pixel-identical claim.
* Neutral sun/sky chromaticity at unchanged energy does not recover the source
  appearance. Broad yellow/lime fills and dark undergrowth remain.
* The tree-only pigment multiplier makes the foliage greener but leaves the
  broad fills, thin coverage and disconnected ground materials. Reject tint
  alone as a solution.
* Blending the world-up lighting normal toward upper-hemisphere mesh normals
  produces more connected light/dark variation in several crowns, without
  changing mesh geometry, source textures or opacity. It is a useful response
  improvement, not a source-parity result or an approved final normal treatment.
* Reversing sun azimuth at the same altitude changes shadows, lit bark and road
  patches, but the original normals still yield broadly filled crowns. The
  normal variant retains more directional crown shading in that reversed case.
  This supports normal response as one contributing cause; it does not isolate
  all remaining coverage, shadowing, LOD or source-environment differences.

Source appearance remains the target
The inventory's original embedded previews look substantially better than the
Unreal scene, including B_001/D_001 already used. Their main-library and bonus
scene raw geometry hashes match. Rebuilding those tree shapes is premature.
Flora F-series and K-series offer stronger conifer forms for later selection,
but transferring a different tree alone would not fix a deficient shader path.

Native Blender 3.4.1 inspection of the two source bonus scenes found:
  GTAO enabled, distance 1.0, factor 1.5; soft shadows enabled; hashed leaf
  transparency and hashed leaf shadows; Standard/None/exposure 0/gamma 1.
The isolated calibration script explicitly disables GTAO and uses a simplified
uniform world and one sun. This confirms a meaningful source-environment
difference that the isolated material benchmark did not establish parity for.
It does NOT yet prove how much GTAO contributes to the attractive source look,
nor that increasing Unreal AO is the right or affordable solution. Test the
source with that feature on/off before adding rendering cost to FloraTrail.
Source light datablocks and compositor nodes were inventoried, but their
presence alone does not prove every light/node participates in the final image.
Neither source file was saved or modified by inspection.

Camera/evidence limits
Road and lower-canopy before/after views are fixed across all cases. Baseline
also has the original upward-crown, mixed-bank and elevated overview captures.
The lower canopy now shows foreground ferns, middle trunks and background, but
the scene remains open; it is not an enclosed forest corridor matching the
reference. The elevated overview is a local test-section regression view, not
a LiDAR valley validation. No modified-case bank/overview or motion pass was
captured. No per-camera exposure, grading, fog or quality changes were made.

Next implementation
First compare representative source trees/ferns using the full source shading
context against the actual Unreal result. Preserve the isolated calibration as
one control, not the complete aesthetic target. Separate coverage/shadow and
environment-occlusion contributions before committing a final normal approach.
Then correct the differing undergrowth response, establish material-family
relationships and selective daylight, and only afterward tune fog/final grade.
Use the source-appearance catalogue to choose coherent additional variants.
Carry the chosen candidate through saved/reloaded images, motion and every
acceptance gate before extending it to LiDAR. Mud/water are untouched here.
