FLORADRIVE / LIDAR MUD + ROCK MASTER TRIAL
=======================================

Scope
-----
Changes are isolated to /Game/GrandTour/ArtTests/FloraDrive. The actual
LiDARStudy map and source materials are read-only inputs. Whole-map adoption
is a later step after reviewing the trial.

Mud reference and transfer
-------------------------
The reference is the saved LiDAR logging mud track, not its firm gravel roads.
Art/Unreal/export_lidar_mud_reference.py exports the actual surface response
settings and samples along the logging road. Art/tools/make_flora_lidar_mud.py
maps the character of stations 170–260 m into the small demonstration route:
dry approach, wet hollow and recovery to firmer ground. It interpolates the
measured depth, softness, wetness and grass cover, and samples the source
corridor composition for litter/churn and road overlay choice.

The surface uses the exact exported LiDAR settings for wheel support, native
substance/water response, shear, rut interaction, grass cover, depth scaling,
maximum depth, bearing strength and settlement speed. The extra demo-specific
tyre-coating override is removed. Soil and tread shaders are local copies of
the actual LiDAR shader paths, with world lookups remapped into this plot.
The native road pigment and tread shading are retained; the demo's previous
strong ground-colour replacement is bypassed on the mud corridor.

Preserved improvements
----------------------
The authored rest-height texture, broken crown, wheel troughs and shoulders
remain. Close POM and matching normal textures remain as surface detail.
The native LiDAR roughness is retained on the road. Soil texture grain adds
restrained modulation rather than replacing the LiDAR mud palette.
Lighting and exposure are held fixed for comparison.

This is a transfer of mud character onto a different road, not a geometrically
identical copy of the LiDAR route. A small number of cells require extra layer
thickness beneath the retained troughs; transfer.json records this adjustment.
The existing pond and puddle topology is retained. CPU soil import and GPU
water/ground bindings share the updated paint data and depth scale.
The import requires a firm boundary; the transfer fades out before the edge.

Rock Master trial
-----------------
Purchased source: D:/GrandTourAssets/FlippedNormals/Rock Master Tools/DATA.blend
Creator: MeiU. Selected Large 005, 053, 077 and Medium 013, 085.
The source materials are baked into colour, tangent normal and roughness
textures. The current conversion uses 1K maps, about 18k triangles per large
mesh and 6.5k per medium mesh, with three Unreal LODs. OpenGL normal maps are
converted by flipping green on import. Source files remain unchanged.
Twenty placements form four embedded exposures with matching smaller rocks;
solid cores clear existing plants and trees. Collision is present and the
driving corridor remains open. A restrained per-material RockTint allows
palette adjustment without rebaking the purchased texture detail.

Rebuild / review
----------------
1. Export the read-only LiDAR mud reference in Unreal.
2. python Art/tools/make_flora_lidar_mud.py
3. Blender --factory-startup -b --python Art/Blender/export_rock_master_trial.py
4. Art/run_flora_drive.ps1 -Stage build
5. Art/review_flora_lidar_trial.ps1 sequences audit, CPU soil verification,
   rendered driving/reset, captures and evidence-bound comparisons.
6. Inspect both comparison images and the individual Unreal screenshots.
Do not run Blender and Unreal concurrently on this machine.

Ground foliage shadow correction / 13 November 2026 UTC
------------------------------------------------------
Cause: build_flora_drive.py enabled shadow maps for trees, rocks, ferns and
shrubs but excluded small broadleaf plants and grasses. This left the broad
leaf silhouettes visibly disconnected from the ground.
The map-local flora_drive_ground_shadows helper now enables dynamic two-sided
casting on 1,153 Small_Plants instances and contact-shadow participation on
near ground foliage. The sun has a 60 cm world-space contact trace, casting
intensity 1.0 and noncasting intensity 0.65. Fine grasses retain their previous
shadow-map policy to contain masked shadow-rendering cost. No source foliage
assets, planting transforms, mud materials, palette or exposure were changed.
Settings: Art/Export/FloraDrive/ground-shadow-settings.json. The main build
reapplies them. Existing maps can use the isolated -Stage shadows operation.

Contact shadows are screen-space and cannot account for off-screen occluders.
The broadleaf shadow-map path supplies real silhouettes; the contact term
adds short nearby grounding. No painted black patch atlas was introduced.
Reference: https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/en-us/unreal-engine/contact-shadows-in-unreal-engine
Local API verified in UE 5.8 LightComponent.h.

LiDAR integration / editability requirements
-------------------------------------------
Current state: local material parameters and JSON/build scripts provide some
controls; there is not yet one consolidated, artist-facing per-map profile.
Before broad migration:
* Appearance: expose palette, transmission, ground detail, contact shadows,
  fog and distance treatments in per-map material instances/settings.
* Placement: use seeded rules plus masks/exclusion zones and named asset-family
  choices. Rebuild affected regions, preserve manual overrides separately.
  Do not copy the demo's delete-and-regenerate tag workflow over hand edits.
* Gameplay: retain LiDAR soil profiles, deformable surfaces and water domains.
  Appearance changes must not silently alter tyre response or layer depths.
* Bindings: initialize PaintWindow, VisualWindow and live field bindings from
  the actual surface/grid; do not inherit coordinate constants from another map.
* Validation: visible SceneDepth change, wheel response, reset and performance
  are separate checks. Keep a representative road/bank review route.
Routine colour/fog/shadow tuning should be easy. Asset replacements or planting
rules require targeted regeneration/reimport and LOD checks. Road/soil/water
changes are the most involved and need drive/deformation/water validation.
Whole LiDARStudy remains unchanged; broad deployment is not performed here.
