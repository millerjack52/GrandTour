Required visual qualities:
- Painterly leaf and grass shapes, overlapping crown masses, and brush-like
  texture variation rather than uniformly noisy or flat foliage.
- Warm sunlit greens/golds and bark, cooler coloured shadows with readable
  detail. Neither washed-out highlights nor almost-black forest interiors.
- Dense, layered ground cover: retain ferns and grass, vary their scale, clump
  shape and species, mix in smaller plants and restrained colour accents.
  Coverage is required, but a repetitive fern carpet is not the style target.
- Cohesive bark, foliage, rocks and ground. Tune retained native vegetation
  to the same visual language as the purchased assets; restoring coverage
  alone does not finish the art direction.
- Cool atmospheric depth separating successive woodland layers without
  turning everything into grey-green haze. Distant canopy retains larger
  readable shapes and light/shadow separation as fine detail falls away.

more uniform old forest. That mismatch is NOT accepted as the intended look.

Why bounded first
The map has about 148,000 native trees. The converted Flora Paint source trees
have tens of thousands of triangles each, including their current lower LODs.
Copying the demo population across the entire forest would be an uncontrolled
increase in rendering and memory cost. This integration uses 145 source trees
at the existing stand positions, with minimum LOD1, plus approximately 6,400
ground plants and 12 Rock Master rocks in three groups. Read install-report.json
for the current exact counts. A whole-map conversion needs cheaper far canopy
representations and streaming/LOD measurements before replacing the remainder.

What changes
- Reviewed Flora Paint materials are cloned under LiDARStudy/FloraIntegration.
  Source textures, geometry, painterly coverage and subtle distance-faded wind
  are retained. Broad leaves, shrubs and ferns cast real shadows. Fine grass
  uses the contact-shadow participation flag and existing scene lighting.
- Native source tree/tall tree-fern instances are removed ONLY in the corridor;
  their transforms are recorded in source-removals.json for regeneration.
  Established low CrownFern/Kiokio ground coverage is retained, except within
  solid outcrop footprints. This corrects the first pass's excessive clearing.
- New living plants remain upright, with roots placed against the final road
  relief. Very steep spots are rejected. Thin low plants use width-based scale
  rather than scaling an almost-flat mesh to an arbitrary large height.
- Rock Master outcrops follow the actual bank slope, are partially buried,
  avoid the driving corridor, and reject new plants through their solid cores.
- Native streamed grass remains in the integrated corridor alongside Flora
  Paint clumps. Only solid outcrop cores mask it out. Its native wheel-contact
  system remains intact. retain_native_grass and retain_ground_ferns default
  to true in the profile and installer, following the owner's coverage request.
- New ground materials wrap the actual approved LiDAR material outputs. They
  retain native WPO, coverage, soil pigment and roughness/wetness. Purchased
  forest-ground colour and normal textures supply grain and normal detail;
  the height texture is reconstructed from normals, NOT a creator height map.
  POM uses 10–24 steps, fades from 7 to 18 metres and branches out beyond the
  section. Other normal/grain detail fades by 65 metres.

Validation and lessons
1. NullRHI checks alone cannot approve a material: the first DX12 pass exceeded
   the native mud material's sampler limit. All seven added detail textures
   now share the engine world-wrap sampler. The runner rejects material compile
   failures or default-material fallback.
2. CPU soil depression and car sinkage are insufficient proof of visible mud.
   The runtime check captures SceneDepth of ONLY the native following grid/tile
   components before/after driving, excluding vehicle, water and tread decals.
   It separately checks native compression and the bound visual-window values.
3. The first 0.5-throttle attempt crossed most of the hollow but did not finish
   the exit within 50 simulation seconds. That attempt is archived under
   HalfThrottle and is not counted as a passing drive. It did demonstrate
   rendered depression (~29.6cm maximum) and new native soil compression.
   The 0.8-throttle retry also failed traversal (station 217.59m at timeout,
   maximum 218.13m); increased wheelspin cut deeper ruts rather than completing
   the exit. Maximum rendered depression was 28.28cm, with 2,846 depth pixels
   changed by more than 1cm. Native compression at the probe increased from
   0.082cm to 6.834cm. Do NOT claim a passed drive or full integration sign-off.
4. Source instance order after restoration must not randomize species on every
   rebuild; sort source positions before deterministic species selection.
5. The before screenshots completed, but that editor process subsequently
   returned an access violation at shutdown. Do not count it as a clean baseline
   test run. The latest 0.8-throttle check also returned a shutdown access
   violation after captures/failed driving assertion. The half-throttle attempt
   exited cleanly. Teardown remains a known validation limitation.
6. Timing samples are hidden-editor tick intervals, NOT GPU timings or a
   standalone performance certification. See the current check report and
   native profiling logs; do not extrapolate this small section to the map.
   Current before/after median tick ms: approach 92.8/90.8, mud 88.7/83.9,
   bank 70.0/67.7, canopy context 38.2/42.4. The canopy sample is slower; cheaper
   distant representations remain necessary before a whole-map replacement.
7. One retained dead spar shared the original hidden-trunk component with
   replaced live trees. Its collision is restored separately by the repair
   stage; future installs remove colliders only at replaced living-tree positions.
   repair-report.json records the location and resulting saved map hash.
   The repair process saved successfully but also had a shutdown access
   violation. A separate fresh-load verify stage subsequently confirmed the
   saved population (145 trees, 6,484 plants, 12 rocks), native terrain/soil,
   water locations, following mud grid, and exactly one restored dead-spar
   collider. Its map hash matches the repair report. This read-only verification
   is separate from the failed traversal gate.
