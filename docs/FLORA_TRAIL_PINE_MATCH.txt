FloraTrail F002 pine: source comparison and coverage correction
=============================================================
14 November 2026. Scope: the existing PineTrial pine only.

Result
The saved Unreal pine is closer to its Blender 3.4.1 source. Foliage groups
retain more of their connected mass and cast closer shadows. This is a measured
coverage calibration, not exact shader parity or acceptance of the whole forest.
The native fine edges remain grainier, and some shadow/lighting differences
remain. No other species or environment palette change is part of this pass.

Installed change
M_Source34_pine_f002 uses a 4096-square raw source coverage mask, preserving the
resolution of the original plant atlas. Its facing-dependent coverage is scaled
by 1.5 before saturation, in both visible and shadow passes. Original pigment,
facing softness, mixed-normal response, 8-frame hash and pixel-animation flag
remain. The leaf still uses native lighting and shadows, with no emissive lift.
The new mask is isolated under PineTrial/SourceMatch/T_SourceMask4K.

The old leaf material is copied to SourceMatch/M_PreMatch. The eight pre-existing
pine packages have a full recoverable backup and SHA-256 manifest at
Art/Export/FloraTrail/PineMatch/baseline.json. Only the leaf material may differ.
Bark, original textures, mesh/LODs, backdrop and pine map remain protected;
the original FloraTrail's 784 files and 90 backup files are verified as well.
No LiDAR, undergrowth, broadleaf, sun, sky, exposure, fog, grading, mud or water
package is modified. The 4K bark and three existing LODs are retained.

Native 768x768 Unreal SceneCapture corrects the earlier projection mismatch.

No AO, fog, Lumen, post grain or per-view grade is used in this isolated bench.

Drivable scene captures retain their original atmosphere and lighting profile.

Diagnosis and selected correction
- Unlit pigment inside 22,468 solid leaf pixels already agrees closely. Mean
  absolute linear RGB error is about (.0024,.0028,.00073). Retinting the pigment
  would therefore hide a different problem.
- Baseline integrated white coverage is about .861 of source, with solid-mask
  intersection-over-union about .831. Leaf groups are visibly thinner/broken.
- Coverage gains 1.25 and 1.5 improve the measured silhouette. The selected 4K
  gain-1.5 candidate reaches about .985 integrated coverage and .949 mask IoU.
  These are opacity measurements, NOT a percent visual match or acceptance score.
- Original source graph sockets/facing formula were checked. A separate up-normal
  diagnostic and three light directions did not justify changing the installed
  mixed normal response. Display/projection and unlit controls separate colour
  transport from coverage differences.
- 128-frame hash cycling, depth decorrelation and forced mip zero did not recover
  the source mass. 4K alone remains about .861: source resolution was worth
  preserving but was not the cause of the density loss. Disabling Has Pixel
  Animation slightly worsened coverage (.854-.855), so it remains enabled.
- Gain on both visible and shadow passes gives closer warm/side lighting than
  a visible-only gain. Back lighting also improves overall, although native
  shadow boundaries still differ. Pigment and scene illumination stay fixed.
- The precise renderer mechanism causing the coverage deficit is not fully
  isolated. This correction is renderer calibration, not a claim to reproduce
  Blender's hashed-alpha implementation exactly.

Evidence and reproduction
Local-only evidence: Art/Export/FloraTrail/PineMatch. Source, before and saved
candidate boards are saved_warm.jpg, saved_side.jpg and saved_back.jpg. PNGs
retain native resolution. Comparison-measurements.json records opacity and
pigment measurements, not visual acceptance. Scene before/after and motion
evidence are generated by Art/review_flora_pine_match.py.

Run one renderer at a time; runners check disk/memory and never close user apps.
After the original licensed pine export and trial exist:
  powershell -File Art/run_flora_pine_match.ps1 -Stage source
  powershell -File Art/run_flora_pine_match.ps1 -Stage mask
  powershell -File Art/run_flora_pine_match.ps1 -Stage candidate
  powershell -File Art/run_flora_pine_match.ps1 -Stage install
  powershell -File Art/run_flora_pine_match.ps1 -Stage recheck
  powershell -File Art/run_flora_pine_match.ps1 -Stage verify
  python Art/review_flora_pine_match.py
Optional probe/sampling/temporal stages retain the original comparison material.
New trial rebuilds apply flora_pine_match_settings.json after source conversion;
they require the local 4K mask bake/report or its already-imported texture.

Validation detail and A-G gate statuses are in
Docs/validation/flora-trail-pine-match.json. The source/mask, candidate, temporal,
install, fresh recheck and full-scene verify processes exited zero. Scene capture
is native 1274x682; it includes fixed road/canopy/bank/valley views and twelve
samples from a 48-frame camera move. Saved package hashes stayed unchanged during
verification. Source coverage and bark both loaded at 4096x4096; 49,314 LOD0
triangles, three LODs and all 193 pine instances were confirmed.
One sampling diagnostic captured
successfully but crashed during shutdown (exit -1073741819); its process is NOT
counted as a pass. Early failed source material-name attempts are retained.
The diagnostic images from that failed shutdown only support rejected hypotheses;
the installed choice is established by successful candidate/install/recheck runs.

Performance remains unverified. Sampled camera movement is not a sustained 60 fps
test or a new driving test of mud and water. This pine improvement does not approve
the remaining palette, other foliage, atmosphere or a LiDAR rollout.
