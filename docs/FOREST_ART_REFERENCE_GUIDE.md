# GrandTour forest art references

Tyler Smith’s sunset forest sets the direction for the view from the pickup. David Holland’s *Meadows* informs how grass, crowns and moss fit together up close. Jake Benbow’s brush patterns offer a way to carry a painterly hand across local surface textures. The trials below use the 42 m LiDAR forest-access bend, with the open paddock and river providing checks at longer distances.

## Jake Benbow — painterly tiling textures

The 2024 article advertised both packs as free. On 23 November 2026, both creator pages listed a base price of £2.50 each. Each volume contains four distinct repeating patterns in colour and greyscale, eight images per volume. The creator permits commercial project use and modification, and prohibits marketplace resale of the textures and their use for training generative AI. [80 Level article](https://80.lv/articles/grab-these-free-painterly-tiling-texture-packs) · [Creator’s first volume](https://jakebenbow.gumroad.com/l/JB_TilingPainterlyTextures_Vol1) · [Creator’s second volume](https://jakebenbow.gumroad.com/l/JB_TilingPainterlyTextures_Vol2)

| Resource | Verified delivery | Swatch worth testing |
|---|---|---|
| [Volume 1](https://jakebenbow.gumroad.com/l/JB_TilingPainterlyTextures_Vol1) | 1,024 px, one ZIP | Second greyscale swatch from the left. Upright blocks could follow a vertical strip of bark. |
| [Volume 2](https://jakebenbow.gumroad.com/l/JB_TilingPainterlyTextures_Vol2) | 2,048 px, one ZIP | Leftmost greyscale swatch. Broad, angled brush marks could break up bare peat between fern colonies. |

The creator specifies colour and greyscale. The public listings leave the image file extension and any dedicated normal, roughness or displacement maps unconfirmed. Benbow’s own example shows how these patterns serve a wider environment. [Club Backrooms](https://www.artstation.com/artwork/m8VKLv)

**First trial.** On the wooded shoulder beside the station 48 m boulder, use the Vol. 2 swatch as a mask between two existing peat pigments. Try a broad and a fine stroke scale at equal contrast. Limit its coverage to exposed earth between the moss colonies. Bake the chosen pattern into a local colour tile at the existing resolution and keep the height, normals and roughness from the existing surface. This changes how the peat is painted without adding a texture lookup while driving. Compare the side camera and pickup approach for visible stroke direction, lost soil detail, sliding, shimmer or a repeating grid. The original material is the control. **Status:** proposed, packs untested.

## David Holland — Meadows

Holland painted simplified plants from real silhouettes and grouped the blades into clumps. A broad colour field carries variation across neighbouring grass. He sculpted canopy masses, distributed leaf cards over them and transferred normals from an enclosing sphere. His statue uses an upward-facing moss mask with vertex-painted adjustments and combines broad baked normals with tiling detail. [Artist’s breakdown](https://80.lv/articles/meadows-creating-stylized-nature-in-ue4) · [Finished scene](https://www.artstation.com/artwork/Ooa2Gk)

**LiDAR trial.** Review the near verge, one broadleaf and the landmark stone independently. Grass should lean in recognisable fans with a shared pigment at their bases. Inspect both faces of the broadleaf cards under side light and compare the whole crown in greyscale at 320 px. On the rock, check whether moss catches ledges while the exposed face retains its mineral shading. On flat ground, use root, shelter and peat patches to locate moss because a slope mask alone covers almost everything. [Jess Hider’s grass construction](https://jesshiderue4.wordpress.com/materials/stylized-wind-blown-grass/) · [Kristóf Lovas’s foliage-normal comparison](https://polycount.com/discussion/209623/smooth-foliage-like-in-breath-of-the-wild-europa-by-helder-pinto-mini-tutorial)

## Tyler Smith — open-world sunset forest

The wide views show irregular dark conifers, cooler distant ridges and warm light concentrated on particular peaks and cloud edges. For the pickup, the comparable moment is the turn opening onto the far bank or paddock. In a separate interview about his earlier Pacific Northwest forest, Smith describes arranging gaps between mid-distance stands, fitting terrain around boulders and giving foliage separate coordinates for individual leaves and a whole-tree gradient. These earlier methods are candidates for recreating the depth seen in the sunset work. [Sunset feature](https://80.lv/articles/open-world-stylized-sunset-forest-scene-made-with-ue5) · [Artist’s sunset project](https://www.artstation.com/artwork/1NOBWq) · [Earlier forest breakdown](https://80.lv/articles/take-a-walk-in-a-stylized-pacific-northwest-forest-visualized-in-3d)

**LiDAR trial.** Redistribute existing visual instances on one approach to reveal a far slope between a near trunk group and a middle stand. Compare both directions in daylight first, then repeat under low side light. Pass when the driver can read the road and three depth layers through the turn, and the tree line holds its shape across LOD changes. Check matched-drive median and 95th-percentile frame time, GPU base pass and shadows. Repeat across launches before attributing a timing difference to the art. [Bend controls and capture instructions](LIDAR_FOREST_ACCESS_BEND.md) · [Sunset study plan](LIDAR_SUNSET_FOREST_DIRECTION.md)

## Tree models for the reference silhouettes

Emma Collins wrote the sunset feature about Tyler Smith’s environment. Smith’s conifers and David Holland’s broadleaf crowns give us two tree shapes to compare. The downloaded libraries contain 273 standing-tree entries across four packs, including seasonal and material variants. The source files for all 17 inspected Blender libraries and scenes are still on disk. The four trees declined during review were Trees Assets Library `Tree_012` and `Tree_021`, and Lazy Forest `Tree_04` and `Tree_05`. [Native beech models and LODs](LIDAR_FOREST_SHAPES.md)

**External alternatives.** Tyler Smith sells an individual windblown tree with the game mesh, foliage cards and bark textures. Its lean offers a distinct landmark silhouette. His store lists a $30 commercial tier for game use. The sunset portfolio piece is separate. Santeri Soininen’s *Stylized Nature Pack* offers nine trees in Unreal and FBX, with painted foliage and a preview showing both pointed conifers and softer broadleaf clumps. It is the stronger mixed-stand fallback if the owned trees clash in daylight. Both external options are untested in GrandTour. [Smith’s tree and licence tiers](https://tsmith3d.artstation.com/store/RpAn/foliage-tree-creation-in-maya-and-zbrush-tutorial-and-assets) · [Soininen’s pack and gallery](https://www.fab.com/listings/f2bf43b5-7eaf-4684-a34b-b615eff37465)

## Earlier project references

**Justin Wallace — foliage lighting.** Wallace’s illustrated UE5 test uses Lumen GI off, disables ray-traced sun and sky shadows, enables world-position-offset evaluation and disables foliage participation in distance-field lighting. He also tunes exposure and calls for subsurface colour and a gradient. GrandTour already shapes crown shading by blending mesh, leaf-card and whole-crown radial normals, then controlling interior occlusion. Its approved Fast shadows and static painted trees give us a different starting point. If dark cards still distract in motion, vary the interior occlusion alone on one broadleaf under the saved daylight. Compare the crown and its shadow on the ground together. [Wallace’s illustrated comparison](https://cdn.80.lv/api/upload/content/df/images/682705b2a4dd3/widen_1840x0.jpeg) · [80 Level article](https://80.lv/articles/experiment-with-these-settings-in-unreal-engine-5-for-stylized-foliage) · [GrandTour crown material](../Art/Unreal/flora_crown_materials.py)

**Romain Durand — water and pebbles.** Durand hand-painted the stones and built a repeating mesh so that cavities could extend underneath overlapping pebbles. His UE5 scene paired it with his own water shader and explored Nanite and Lumen. The LiDAR pebble patch is retired. The transferable observation concerns exposed rock on land: an undercut that should read at a grazing camera angle needs a corresponding shape in the mesh. Use the existing landmark boulder to judge that silhouette from the pickup. [Durand’s project](https://www.artstation.com/artwork/o2BbJz) · [80 Level article and artist’s explanation](https://80.lv/articles/ue5-powered-stylized-water-pebbles)
