Original saved-map mud comparison (before the current transfer)
-------------------------------------------------------------
FloraDrive inherits MudRoad's demo setup, not the LiDAR configuration.
Both use GTLandscapeSurface with wheel support, soil shear response, rut
interaction and native mud shader updates enabled. The core system is shared.

Confirmed differences:
* Ground/tread materials: FloraDrive M_FloraSoil/M_FloraTread; LiDAR
  CohesiveStyle/M_EvergreenDetail and M_Tread. Both ground shaders are Default
  Lit, but the LiDAR tread is Unlit while FloraDrive tread is Default Lit.
  FloraDrive adds purchased ground textures, reconstructed POM height,
  authored road-mask colour/roughness and the newly shaped rest-height bed.
* Soil character: different paint/rest maps and road authoring. The four
  established LiDAR gravel roads have firm_gravel=true and mud_depth_cm=0.
  Its saved logging mud track has base mud_depth_cm=5 (conditions can override
  local values). FloraDrive is a deliberately yielding, rutted test route.
* Grass handling: grass_is_visual_cover=false in FloraDrive, true in LiDAR.
  In the shared code, grass cover can reduce settlement and cutting when
  false; it does not provide that reinforcement when true. This can change
  driving response in grassy areas even if the visible texture is similar.
* Presentation scale: FloraDrive has a fixed visual grid on a 63 m paint
  domain; LiDAR follows the player across a 3.7 km paint domain. Those paint
  extents/resolutions are authoring data, not a direct physics-grid comparison.
* Tyre presentation: FloraDrive explicitly assigns M_ProvingTyre; LiDAR has
  no surface-level tyre coating override. This is not itself a grip setting.
* Water integration: FloraDrive's native visual actor binds water/sediment
  update materials. The equivalent LiDAR actor has these slots empty and
  extend_water_response_to_domain=true on its surface. LiDAR has its own
  water integration; empty slots here do not imply all LiDAR water is absent.

Conclusion: appearance differs and some response settings differ. This audit
does not quantify a matched driving/traction delta. Before map integration,
pick a representative LiDAR mud section and compare identical vehicle/input,
soil condition and lighting, then carry over the chosen visual improvements
while preserving the intended LiDAR gameplay settings.
