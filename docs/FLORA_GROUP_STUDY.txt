Reference
Casey Sheep / Flora Paint forest-path image supplied by the owner: tall pines,
golden sunlight on the right, cooler shade, layered plants around a path.
The purchased 001.blend scene supplies the frozen path geometry, placement and
camera. This study remaps its foliage variants to six source-converted samples:
fern, flowering shrub, Tree_B_001, Tree_D_001, Grass_001 and Dry_Grass_001.
It is a reduced plant palette, not a claim that the full pack is converted.
Ground, rocks, fallen litter and twigs reuse earlier scenic materials.
Original assets and artist reference are local licensed dependencies.

What is being tested
Same geometry and camera under warm sun/cool sky, sky only, sun plus a warm
spot and cool point light, low sky with local lights off, and local lights on.
A separate native UE Two Sided Foliage diagnostic retains the same pigment
and masks. This comparison exposes the sun-oriented lighting approximation;
it is not presented as an equivalent Blender shader or a chosen replacement.
Local lights use explicit candela units and inverse-square falloff. See:
https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/using-physical-lighting-units-in-unreal-engine

Bark correction
The first group pass regressed to a whole-tree 2K atlas. It lost the dedicated
bark packing used in the previous FloraPath work and looked visibly blurry.
Bark now has independent UV1 packing and a 4K BC7/sRGB bake of the ACTIVE source
procedural wood-and-moss shader. Leaf UV0/UV1 and geometry hashes are unchanged.
Both tree types retain authored geometry normals. This source bark graph has
NO connected normal-map/bump input: the older note that source bark normals
still needed transferring was an assumption, corrected by this graph audit.
The visible 3.jpg image branch is inactive and must not replace the active
procedural wood shader. No invented normal texture was added.
Each trunk still uses one colour texture sample; foliage remains unchanged.
The dedicated bark images are not an excuse to increase all foliage textures.

Texture checks
The reopen audit enumerates every instanced mesh/material slot and texture.
New foliage colour and coverage bindings are verified by hashes of the exact
source-generated files. Bark maps must retain their 4096 cap and reach 4096
after load warmup. Colour inputs use sRGB; mask inputs use linear sampling.
Earlier scenery records its actual imported file paths and settings; it is not
mislabelled as newly source-matched. Material fallback assignments fail audit.
Blender material names can acquire .001 suffixes and FBX replaces dots with
underscores. Binding accepts unique sanitized identities, never array order.

Display and captures
SceneCapture's sRGB target uses linear source output; the editor backbuffer
requires explicit Standard/sRGB encoding when replacing its tonemapper.
The saved map therefore uses M_ViewportStandard. The scene-capture loop uses
the original linear output pass. No image grading or generated imagery is used
in the comparison boards. Editor screenshots wait 64 frames for temporal AA.
Audit tick callbacks are guarded against re-entry during shader compilation.

Findings / limits
The group reveals excessive canopy darkness and weak/uneven local-light leaf
response compared with bark and ground. Native foliage lighting responds more
strongly, but does not preserve the same source appearance. The next shader
step is a light-independent multi-lobe model, validated under these same rigs,
not another global colour gain. Fine stochastic edge noise also remains.
The study has 1,880 instances in 31 components; 1,236 instances use the six new
foliage meshes. LOD0 upper bound is 3,217,992 triangles, not measured draw cost.
There is one shadow-casting spot and one unshadowed point fill. Unreal reports
a non-Nanite virtual-shadow marking workload warning. This is an optimisation
item, and FPS/60fps are NOT verified. No LiDAR or original FloraPath promotion.
