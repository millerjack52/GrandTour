FLORA TRAIL / SOURCE FOLIAGE + DEFORMABLE MUD + WATER

Scope
A separate small playable section using the approved FloraGroup source conversion
and the established FloraDrive terrain/vehicle/water simulation. It does not
replace the LiDAR map or overwrite either approved study. The 52 m S-shaped
route has three recovery points, a muddy dip and a woodland pond beside it.

Drive
Run Drive-FloraTrail.bat. W/S accelerate/brake, A/D steer, Space handbrake.
1/2/3 recover to entry/mud/water. Backspace resets the section and soil.
F1 toggles the driving diagnostic overlay.

Rebuild
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File Art/run_flora_trail.ps1 -Stage build
powershell -ExecutionPolicy Bypass -File Art/run_flora_trail.ps1 -Stage check
Requires the local purchased pack assets and previously generated study assets;
these licensed binary inputs are not redistributed in the source repository.

Visual principles
Six source-converted families: pine, broadleaf tree, fern, shrub, grass and dry
grass. Preserve source pigments, opacity and geometric normals, including the
separate 4K wood/moss bake. Remap the seeded clustered layout by object bounds,
keeping root heights, open running lane, short verges, taller understory and
background tree layers. Consolidate instances by family and near/far usage.
Ground and rock materials retain existing source maps, relief and normals.
Map-local material copies and lighting controls keep changes isolated.

Controls
Art/flora_trail_settings.json: sun direction, source sun/sky strengths and
colours, fog density/colour, sky/cloud luminance and rendering settings.
Source foliage lighting uses the map-local MPC_SourceLighting; keep its sun
vector and sky radiance synchronized with the scene lights via the builder.
The Standard display transfer must remain enabled in the game viewport.

Mud and water contract
Keep LiDAR physical soil parameters, the native mud update, real grid vertex
displacement, sediment/water state and changing water-bed updates. Keep the
local MR_VisualWindow at (0,0,3750,600) on ground AND tread materials. POM adds
small surface relief; it is not the tyre deformation. The established regression
measures actual NativeVisualGrid SceneDepth before/after driving, excludes the
car/water/tread overlays, and separately verifies reset and GPU shader oracles.

Limits
The analytical foliage shader matches source sun plus uniform sky; general
headlight/point-light parity is still unresolved. This daylight section is not
a claim of a full-pack conversion or a 60 fps production release. Source trees
and grass may still show temporal edge noise. Driving captures with pauses are
functional evidence, not a clean performance benchmark.

Validation
See docs/validation/flora-trail.json and local Art/Export/FloraTrail results.

Surface palette bridge
The converted plants keep their source pigments. The surrounding native terrain
uses a local albedo adjustment to fit the Standard display: road 0.8, verge 0.42,
Rock Master surfaces 0.65. Water retains its native opacity, wave normals, vertex
displacement and sediment state. A soft mask within the existing pond adjusts
its colour, scattering and absorption towards a cool green-blue; road puddles
retain the muddy medium. PondTint is exposed on the local M_FloraWater material
and in flora_trail_settings.json. This is a visual adapter, not changed physics.

Final verification (2026-11-13)
Build, saved-asset/visual capture and continuous driving check exited 0. Source
maps and approved foliage assets retained their hashes. All populated foliage
components use the approved texture bindings; bark loaded at 4096x4096. There
are 10,042 foliage instances in eight components, with three LODs per mesh.

The car drove 51.29 m, contacting water up to 19.34 cm deep. SceneDepth of the
actual ground grid measured up to 7.53 cm of depression and 3.33 cm of raised
soil; 1,199 pixels changed by more than 1 cm. The fixed terrain probes returned
to their initial heights with zero error on reset. Native GPU shader oracles,
water-bed updates and sediment targets passed. The pond bed is -31.99 cm; the
minimum tested road crown-to-trough relief is 16.33 cm.

Two 300-frame stationary PIE spot checks at 1274x682 gave GPU medians of 16.07 ms
(road) and 14.61 ms (pond), with p95 of 21.42 / 18.85 ms. These are not a 1080p,
packaged-game or sustained-driving 60 fps validation. Captures show remaining
foliage edge noise and sparse distant crown coverage, which need a dedicated
rendering/LOD refinement pass before a wider rollout.

Generated map rebuilds keep a map backup under Saved/FloraTrailBackups.
Art/review_flora_trail.py assembles unretouched screenshots and checks that all
reports refer to the same saved asset fingerprint before writing validation.
