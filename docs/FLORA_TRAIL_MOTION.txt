FloraTrail foliage motion and falling-leaf study
==============================================
16 November 2026. STATUS: testing held at the user's explicit request.
The source implementation is prepared; no finished motion preview or performance
result exists yet. No driving launcher was changed and LiDAR is untouched.

Intent
Keep the approved painterly palette and the ground candidate, add gentle leaf
motion and a small amount of falling foliage, and measure the additional cost.
Base: Woodland/GroundPatches/v2/L_FloraTrail_GroundPatches.
Target: Woodland/Motion/v2/L_FloraTrail_Motion (not generated yet).

Foliage wind
Clone only masked tree, pine, fern, shrub, grass and dry-grass materials.
Keep pigment, mask, normals, roughness, subsurface, AO and texture bindings;
change only the world-position-offset output. Preserve original assets and LODs.
Use a height-rooted low-frequency gust plus spatially varied flutter. Maximum
amplitude control is 3 cm for canopy, 1.5-2 cm for understory. Roots stay still.
Canopy bark remains static; the small leaf offset is not branch skeleton motion.
The candidate replaces the old tiny whole-surface breeze rather than adding
another breeze on top. The comparison's baseline restores original materials.
Wind fades from 20 to 45 metres, with component WPO disabled beyond 50 metres.
Displacement is bounded to 8 cm in the material. No added texture samples.
Shadow-depth passes use zero new offset; existing rigid shadow caching remains.
This deliberately keeps small flutter out of moving canopy shadows, avoiding
continuous virtual-shadow cache invalidation. Inspect the visual trade-off.

Falling leaves
72 fixed instances in six small groups along the road; one instanced component.
Use the existing engine plane and a single isolated leaf from the original 4K
Flora source coverage atlas. Its inspected UV rectangle is (.326,.019) to
(.398,.106). No purchased texture is altered or committed. The first inspected
rectangle clipped the shape and was corrected before successful material creation.
Olive/ochre/restrained coral pigment stays within the scene's established roles.
Shader time supplies 13-19 second cycles, downward travel, lateral drift and
tumbling. Instances are 10-17 cm plane size; the cutout is smaller within that.
Spawn/expiry opacity ramps hide the loop reset. No accumulated ground debris.
No collision, particle lights, cast shadows, ray tracing, distance-field lighting
or per-leaf CPU tick. The effect is an instanced shader animation, not Niagara
simulation. It is bounded and placed locally; it does not follow the player or
create particles across the whole world. It also does not collide with branches.
Shader fade covers 18-32 metres horizontally; component culling ends at 45 metres.
The 1250 cm WPO bound accommodates the fall and drift. This expanded bound is a
trade-off to verify for culling; 72 leaves is a cap, not a measured GPU budget.

Controls and review
Art/flora_motion_settings.json holds distances, leaf count and timing duration.
FloraWindCm/FloraWindEnabled control wind; FloraTimeOverride=-1 uses real time.
Nonnegative overrides exist only for reproducible capture at chosen timestamps.
Compare original materials/leaves hidden, wind only, and wind plus leaves.
Fixed road, bank, canopy, valley and leaf-facing cameras use the same lighting.
Capture 48 actual Unreal frames at 12 animation samples/second over four seconds.
This deterministic sequence is a preview, not a statement about render FPS.
review_flora_motion.py encodes native-resolution MP4 and a reduced GIF.

Performance protocol
Separate from screenshots, unpause simulation and run real shader/game time.
At a fixed camera, record CSV for baseline/wind/both/both/wind/baseline, ten
seconds each, with six seconds settling before each window and no screenshots
inside a timing window. Baseline restores original materials, not just zero
wind strength, so it does not retain candidate vertex-program cost.
Use the existing CSV analyzer to retain frame/GPU/game-thread/render-thread
distributions, raw captures and memory warnings. Compare both repeated means
and p95. Small differences may be noise; do not claim zero cost from small deltas.
This is uncooked PIE/offscreen screening, not Shipping or a sustained driving
60 fps certification. A moving-camera/vehicle check and broader visibility
conditions remain necessary before a production performance claim.
