# Fun tests

The three hard gates from PROJECT_PLAN.md section 8. Each one: play for the set time with a
controller, without the code or the docs open, then answer each question in a line. A test passes
when the pass questions are a plain yes; if not, we iterate there before M5. The review goes under
the sheet, written with the user.

Saved/SaveGames is empty (2026-09-14), so the first play starts a fresh campaign. The editor
opens L_TestTrack; the other maps are under Content/GrandTour.

## Fun test #1 – driving (M1)

Play: L_TestTrack, 15 minutes. Cross tarmac, dirt, mud and snow. Hit something on purpose. Run a
part or the tank down if you can.

1. Did you want to keep driving when the 15 minutes were up? (pillar 1; pass)
2. Could you tell the four surfaces apart with your hands, not your eyes? (pass)
3. After the hit, did the car feel different, and could you see why from the seat? (pass)
4. What on the dash did you read, and what did you never look at?
5. One thing that annoyed you. One thing that was good.

## Fun test #2 – the stop-and-work moment (M2)

Play: L_OnFootTest, 15 minutes. Drive, stop, get out, work a node with a tool, strip a wreck, get
bogged on purpose and winch out, fix a part in the field, put the haul in the boot, drive off.
Harvesting is a placeholder: judge the moment of stopping and working, not the nodes or the yields.

1. Was getting out a decision or a chore? (pillar 1; pass)
2. Did the tools feel like tools in your hands (the hold, the aim, the bar) or like a menu?
3. The winch: did rigging it on foot and reeling out feel earned? (pass)
4. Did you know what you had and where it was, bag or boot, without thinking?
5. One thing that annoyed you. One thing that was good.

## Fun test #3 – the core gamble (M3)

Play: start at L_Base, read the briefing board, drive out through the gate and run L_Leg01:
checkpoints, the weather front, the radiation pocket, stops for loot, extraction, the results card,
back to the base. Two runs, about 20 minutes. On the second run push a stop longer than felt safe.

1. Did you ever weigh one more stop against the weather or the timer? (pillar 2; pass)
2. Did the briefing change what you did?
3. Did the exposure bar send you back to the car, or did you ignore it?
4. Did the results card read as the story of the run, or as numbers?
5. Did you want to go again, and what would you do differently? (pillar 3; pass)
6. One thing that annoyed you. One thing that was good.

## Tour hand test (M5, 2026-09-14)

Play: a fresh campaign from the camp (delete Saved/SaveGames/GrandTour0.sav for leg 1), read the
board, drive the five legs, watch the results card each time and the ending after leg 5. What
helps, per leg:

1. Your time against par and the limit (the pars were guessed from length; this is the curve).
2. Did the mission read from the board and the HUD before you did it? The moment you were unsure.
3. The bear: did the brief get you there, did the flight feel fair, the range and the hold.
4. The rivals: plausible placings or the same one always ahead; did points or prize money change
   a decision at the camp.
5. The results card and the ending: what you read first, skipped, missed.
6. Layout: an unclear route, a crossing that stopped the car dead, off the floor, a wrong surface.
7. Tone: the briefs and the ending lines, keep / cut / one line rewritten your way.
8. Bugs: the leg, what you were doing, what happened; Saved/Logs/GrandTour.log after the session.
9. One word per leg (fun, fine, dull) and the one moment worth keeping.

## Reviews

### 2026-09-14 – the tour, all five legs

The user drove all five legs: "As a base systems test, it's fine." Beyond that no meaningful
feedback is possible: this is an adventure driving game, and the maps are completely flat with
no visuals. Taken as the verdict on the greybox phase as a whole: M0 to M5 work as systems, and
the next thing that can be judged needs terrain with shape. The tour sheet above stays for the
first legs with real ground.

### 2026-09-14 – all three, one sitting

The user played #1, #2 and #3 and gave one verdict: "Looking solid for an early version." No
per-question answers and nothing named to change, so all three gates pass on that verdict and M5
starts. The questions above stay for the next round, after M5 or the first real leg set, when the
answers should be taken one at a time.
