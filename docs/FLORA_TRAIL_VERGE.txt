FloraTrail: woodland floor at the road edge
==========================================

Scope
The pale green ground strip beside the mud read as a smooth carpet. This pass
targets that exposed floor, based on the working interpretation of the user's
"grassy patch" feedback. It does not establish acceptance of the grass meshes:
the dark, speckled grass and shrub coverage remains visibly unresolved.

Implementation
L_FloraTrail_Verge is a separate candidate derived from the flat-water mud map.
M_VergeLandscape and M_VergeMud share the same woodland-floor colour treatment;
MI_VergeMud retains the wet mud instance's parameters. The wrapper reuses the
existing POM-aligned forest soil, moss and litter samples. It adds no texture
samples or geometry, and changes no source texture or foliage LOD.

The floor blends from exposed soil toward restrained moss and warm leaf litter.
The transition begins outside the central wheel channels, at 215-310 cm from
the curved road centre, varied by up to 20 cm by a broad noise field. Earlier
290-430 cm bounds left the old pale band exposed and were rejected. Both the
underlying terrain and native overlay use the new relationship because updating
the terrain alone did not produce a visible improvement in the review frame.

Mud vertex displacement, pixel depth offset and mask programs are retained and
checked against the wet baseline. The saved M_WaterFlat binding remains intact.
The new ground material changes only colour, normal response and roughness at
the verge. The central mud treatment, physical deformation and water simulation
remain the established implementation. Lighting, fog and exposure are unchanged.

Reproduction
With other Unreal/Blender renderers closed, from C:/GrandTour run sequentially:
  powershell -File Art/run_flora_verge.ps1 -Stage probe
  powershell -File Art/run_flora_verge.ps1 -Stage candidate
  powershell -File Art/run_flora_verge.ps1 -Stage verify
  python Art/review_flora_verge.py

Probe temporarily hides the floor forms, distant surround, apron and native
grid separately. It saves no assets and captures two fixed views per case.
Candidate installs the isolated material/map copies and captures baseline and
woodland-floor controls from six fixed views plus eight sampled motion frames.
It waits for source texture loading and freezes simulation for the comparison.
Verify reloads the saved map, checks its bindings and controls, captures again,
and runs the existing physical driving test without resaving any package.

Evidence and recovery
Art/Export/FloraTrail/VergeStudy contains native captures, colour/greyscale review
boards, source-reference comparison, sampled motion and process reports.
Docs/validation/flora-trail-verge.json records the measured results and limits.
The initial read-only NullRHI inventory exited with a shutdown access violation;
it is diagnostic context, not a passing renderer result. Two initial candidate
attempts failed automation checks (private API access, then save-as to an already
existing map); those logs remain under first-candidate and save-retry. The
terrain-only and wide-transition candidates are retained as rejected studies.
An added preservation check initially referenced a hidden Unreal enum; the
corrected check compares the public vertex/mask outputs and every original
custom shader program. The final comparison completed its capture/assertions
but exited with a late shutdown access violation; this remains a failed
process result under shutdown-failed. Fresh-load verification is a separate
process and must be reported independently, never substituted into that log.

The baseline helper protects all 43 pre-existing MudTrial packages and verifies
the previous source/pine/LiDAR baseline chain. Copies are held on D: alongside
the earlier backups. L_FloraTrail_MudFlat remains the recoverable prior scene.
Earlier MudTrial review media moved to D:/GrandTourAssets/ReviewEvidence to
restore render space; every file was hash-checked and a junction preserves the
original C:/GrandTour/Art/Export/FloraTrail/MudTrial paths.

Limits
This is a ground-surface correction, not a complete grass or forest-style pass.
Existing dark plant coverage, bright green stones, canopy colour and material
cohesion still need work. Sampled motion is not full temporal acceptance and
no sustained 60 fps claim is made. Palette A-G remain separately assessed;
passing technical checks does not mean the scene matches the references.

of wheel water contact and 5.54 cm of measured mud height change. All 47

and the protected baseline chain remained unchanged. Graph texture samples
