FloraTrail woodland: browner mud / 15 November 2026
=================================================

Requested change: make the mud a bit more brown.
The road pigment alone is multiplied in linear colour by [1.12, 0.82, 0.60],
before the existing moisture darkening and road/forest-floor blend. The
original colour texture's variation is retained. Wet sheen, roughness, normal
detail, parallax relief, displacement, deformation and flat water are unchanged.
No light, fog, exposure, vegetation or rock change is included.

The existing Woodland launcher opens the new map after verification:
  /Game/GrandTour/ArtTests/FloraTrail/Woodland/BrownMud/L_FloraTrail_BrownMud
The preceding Woodland/v3 map and all its 27 packages remain unchanged and
backed up. Earlier baselines, source assets and LiDAR are also protected.
Only the new map's GTMudRunnerVisual uses MI_WoodlandBrownMud; its parent
copies the original M_VergeMud and inserts the pigment multiplier into the
road colour input. Original scalar/texture overrides and normal, roughness
and displacement output connections are audited.

Reproduce from C:/GrandTour, one renderer at a time:
  powershell -File Art/run_flora_woodland_mud.ps1 -Stage candidate
  powershell -File Art/run_flora_woodland_mud.ps1 -Stage verify
  python Art/review_flora_woodland_mud.py
Settings: Art/flora_woodland_mud_settings.json.

The candidate capture uses identical paused deformation, cameras and lighting
with neutral and brown pigment multipliers. Verify opens the saved map in a
fresh process and checks the stored multiplier without setting it at runtime.
Native capture size is 1274 x 682. Source textures are awaited before capture.
This pigment edit does not establish a new driving, motion or performance pass
and does not change the broader woodland palette gate assessment.
