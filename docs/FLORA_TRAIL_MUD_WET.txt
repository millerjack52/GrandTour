FLORATRAIL WET MUD RESPONSE - 14 NOVEMBER 2026
============================================

Problem and cause
The chosen Brown Mud 02 surface had readable clods but looked predominantly
dry beside its puddles. Its imported roughness has median 0.996, 10th percentile
0.835 and 90th percentile 1.0. The comparison wrapper gave this map 65% of final
roughness, overwhelming much of the previous ground's wet response. The colour
modulation alone was insufficient to establish wet mud.

Controlled tests and decision
Compared baseline, darker colour only, lower roughness only, and a combined
wet response under identical lighting, exposure, cameras, textures and frozen
soil/water state. Lower roughness supplies the missing directional sheen;
darkening alone still looks dry. The combined response is the selected candidate.

New map: /Game/GrandTour/ArtTests/FloraTrail/MudTrial/L_FloraTrail_MudWet
New material: M_MudTrial_Wet / MI_MudTrial_brown_wet in the same folder.
Drive-FloraTrail-Mud.bat opens this wet candidate after verification.
Drive-FloraTrail-Mud-Dry.bat retains the previous Brown Mud 02 comparison map.
The 38 original MudTrial packages are unchanged and backed up on D:; the
manifest is Art/Export/FloraTrail/MudWet/baseline.json. Original FloraTrail,
PineTrial, the source diagnostic, bark/LODs and LiDAR remain protected.

What changed
Wetness is driven by the native material's existing live/rest ground height
and wetness value, with local cavity variation from the same 4K relief texture.
Raised clods remain rougher than lower ground. Wet colour darkens with moisture;
fine normal strength is partially reduced where the sheen is strongest.
Native large-scale normals, POM height, geometry, collision and deformation
remain intact. The standing-water and tyre-mark materials are unchanged.

Controls in Art/flora_mud_wet_settings.json:
  DampRough 0.48, FilmRough 0.16, Darken 0.48, NormalKeep 0.55.
These are endpoints blended through the height/cavity/moisture masks, not
uniform roughness or colour values across the road. The source roughness map
still contributes restrained local variation. This is an artistic wet-surface
response using native state, not a newly simulated water-film transport model.
No changes to sunlight, sky, exposure, grading or the 4K texture sources.

Visual assessment
Wetness: pass in the close oblique view; patchy reflective highlights now cross
the clods instead of appearing only on the separate puddles. Straight-ahead
driving views show a subtler damp sheen, as expected from their viewing angle.
Relief: pass; clod shape and detailed texture remain legible under the sheen.
Restraint: partial; a directly sun-facing patch produces strong pale highlights.
It remains textured, but this is not a claim of complete painterly acceptance.
Distance: partial; broad ruts remain the primary long-range shape signal.
Motion: partial; sampled movement preserves pattern/shape, but does not prove
full temporal stability at all angles. Sustained 60 fps is not tested.

Whole-scene palette gates are not promoted by this surface correction:
A fail (vegetation hierarchy), B partial (crown masses), C partial (shade),
D fail (whole material ensemble), E not tested (required forest depth layers),
F partial (two-scale painterly/temporal read), G not tested (complete review set).
Prior scene failures remain unresolved; no LiDAR rollout is approved here.

Reproduction
Art/run_flora_mud_wet.ps1 -Stage probe builds the isolated clone and compares
the four controls using the established mud capture harness.
-Stage saved applies settings and performs a drive if a later profile is chosen.
-Stage verify performs the fresh read-only load, captures and physical traversal;
it checks all trial package hashes before/after. The original dry trial and pine
baseline checks run before and after every process. One renderer at a time;
no unrelated processes are closed. Art/review_flora_mud_wet.py creates the boards.
Source/configuration/documentation only are pushed; asset files stay local.
