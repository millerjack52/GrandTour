REFERENCE ART-DIRECTION PASS / 2026-11-13
=======================================
Request: try another reference pass, including less conventional techniques.

Diagnosis
The surviving mismatch is a hierarchy of forms: individual cards have equal
visual weight, lower trunks are unbroken vertical bars, and neighbouring crowns
vary independently rather than forming painted masses. The reference has dark
plant centres, warm tips, moss on bark and groups of related vegetation colour.

Chosen experiment: instance-space pigment volumes
- Normalize each source mesh against INSTANCE local bounds before wind. Feed
  this through a vertex interpolator, preserving identity in large HISM groups.
- Paint a restrained cool root-to-tip value gradient inside plant clumps. Blend
  source normals toward a clump dome to shade the volume while retaining fronds.
- Give exposed upper growth an ochre tint. Add localized rust colonies rather
  than globally warming all foliage or lifting the scene's exposure.
- Break lower trunks with vertically irregular moss patches, preserving the
  purchased bark underneath. Use real lighting; no emissive moss/leaf highlights.
- Use a slow world field shared by neighbouring crowns to form colour families.
  Apply the same family field to near geometry and far cards.
- Reconnect transmission to final pigment so it cannot bypass the new shading.

Why this route
It changes object appearance in 3D, not a screen-space blur that can smear
silhouettes or swim during camera motion. It adds no meshes, shadow casters or
texture samples. Extra shader arithmetic/interpolators still have a cost; zero
samples does not imply free, and 60fps is not established by this experiment.
Lighting, fog, cameras, render resolution, mud and planting counts stay fixed
for a fair comparison. The existing map is backed up before modification.

Source checks
- Purchased FloraPath/Implementation-notes.txt: the source diffuse lobe uses
  mesh normals; the custom Flora_Shader normal affects transmission. A general
  upward normal blend is not equivalent. Keep moderate volume normal blending.
- UE5.8 MaterialExpressions.cpp and HLSLMaterialTranslator.cpp: Bounds defaults
  to InstanceLocal, distinct from the potentially huge HISM ObjectLocal bounds.
- Epic shading-model documentation describes Two Sided Foliage transmission:
  https://dev.epicgames.com/documentation/unreal-engine/shading-models-in-unreal-engine

Additional research
Valve's Illustrative Rendering in Team Fortress 2 describes warm/cool form
shading and perspective-attached painted detail. Those are useful principles,
not a request to copy TF2's look. Here they are applied to the supplied Flora
Paint reference through plant volumes and existing lit Unreal materials.
https://cdn.steamstatic.com/apps/valve/2007/NPAR07_IllustrativeRenderingInTeamFortress2.pdf
