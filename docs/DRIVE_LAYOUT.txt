DRIVE LAYOUT CONVERSIONS — 2026-10-28

Controls
Stop the car, press U, select DRIVE LAYOUT (or cycle with Tab), choose using
arrows/mouse, then Enter / Fit. U returns to driving. Preview, Compare and Cancel
do not change the drivetrain. Range Rover retains all workshop categories;
other fleet cars expose the drive-layout category only.

AWD torque split (2026-10-28)
With AWD selected, Left/Right or the on-screen arrow buttons adjust the nominal
front/rear split in 5% steps, from 10:90 to 90:10. Left adds rear bias. Up/Down
still choose the drive layout. Enter applies both layout and split; Cancel/Close
discard preview changes. Retuning an already fitted AWD kit requires no extra
kit, consumes no inventory and does not repair damage. The car must be stopped.
50:50 is the default; 30:70 is an available rear-biased setup.

Choices
Factory: uses the car's original CentreSplitFront (Ferrari RWD, Range Rover AWD).
FWD: front axle receives drive; rear axle receives no drive or diff coupling.
RWD: rear axle receives drive; front axle receives no drive or diff coupling.
AWD: adjustable front/rear torque before differential coupling (default 50:50).

Conversion is independent of the engine package, gear ratios, suspension and
tyres. Range Rover Standard/Rally/Expedition combinations remain available.
Front/rear shaft-speed weighting, driven contacts and reflected engine inertia
follow the selected wheels. Selectable locks only couple driven axles. Ferrari
limited slip follows the driven axle(s). Handbrake retains the existing automatic
clutch release and rear wheel brakes; it does not become a front axle brake in FWD.

Storage and fitting
Optional Slot.DriveLayout in the existing mechanical loadout. Parts are
Part.DriveFactory, Part.DriveFWD, Part.DriveRWD, Part.DriveAWD. Old saves without
this slot retain Factory. The slot migrates onto existing blueprint instances
at BeginPlay. No map, vehicle blueprint or wheel asset rewrite is required.

The AWD split is an independent setting on the drivetrain, copied by the existing
campaign capture/apply path through FGTCarrySnapshot.AWDFrontShare. Older saves
default to 50:50. It is remembered while switching layouts but only affects an
explicit AWD conversion. Factory, FWD and RWD retain their normal axle selection.
The split also weights shaft RPM and reflected engine inertia. Centre coupling
can redistribute torque away from the nominal split while conserving total drive.

Campaign uses the normal carry snapshot/save path. Workshop fitting in the rally
test lab is free, changes the active car and does not auto-save a campaign or
persist a lab selection across a fresh launch. Campaign fitting uses a conversion
kit from the boot and returns the old kit. Conversion items have no added mass or
performance multiplier in this prototype; the change is driven-wheel topology.

Only Range Rover and Ferrari use the existing independent engine/clutch and firm
ground wheel-momentum model. Other cars keep their existing engine model. Explicit
conversions conserve shaft torque through the diffs; Factory retains the fleet's
legacy torque-cap behavior where applicable.

Validation
Art/workshop.ps1 -Mode LayoutCheck runs native disk-serialization checks and
rendered PIE tests on the existing L_RallyHandling map. It tests Range Rover,
Ferrari and BMW conversions, forward/reverse axle torque, lock turns, driven
shaft RPM, low range and handbrake (RR/Ferrari), stationary-only fitting, previews,
Factory restoration, old-loadout compatibility, recreation from captured loadout,
engine-package coexistence, and protected map/player-save hashes.

differential, reverse/lock, disk save and production snapshot capture/apply tests,
then rendered driving at five splits on Range Rover and Ferrari. It also checks

engine-package coexistence and protected save/map hashes.
