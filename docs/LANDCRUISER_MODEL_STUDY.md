# Land Cruiser 80 — rebuilt Blender asset

## September 30 — new stylized exterior study

After accepting `Art/Export/VehicleTools/reference-wagon-v03/`, the user requested a Land Cruiser in that same simple shape language. The fresh study is `Art/Export/VehicleTools/stylized-landcruiser-v02/Stylized_LandCruiser80.blend`, generated by `Art/VehicleTools/build_stylized_landcruiser.py`. This preserves all earlier Blender and Unreal assets below and does not replace their packaged or rigged files.

The 80-series identity follows the existing project references, translated into broad planes, a taller upright cabin, a nearly vertical tail, charcoal flares and lower cladding, larger tyres, horizontal grille and ivory paint. Artistic dimensions differ from the earlier factory-scale study. The accepted wagon and Land Cruiser concept are packed into the new blend. The source snapshot and `spec.json` sit beside it.

Six actual Blender views cover front/side/top, both three-quarter angles and an arch/door/running-board close-up. Revision 02 cuts the bumper intakes and wheel vents into their surfaces, trims the side mouldings away from bumper corners and raises the simplified underbody mass. `static-fit-report.json` checks finite coordinates and static tyre-shell triangle intersections with body/flares and running boards. It does not check tread, containment, steering or suspension movement. This is an exterior style study with interior masses; no new rig, UVs, collision, export, functional cockpit or Unreal work is included. The user responded positively to revision 02 and requested a Range Rover in the same style.

Real-vehicle identity source: [Toyota's 1992–1995 exterior gallery](https://media.toyota.co.uk/images/land-cruiser-vx-exterior-1992-1995/).

## Shape and material changes

Rebuilt the side skins around shared cross sections, domed the bonnet, rolled the nose, and replaced tubular arch trim with broad body-coloured flares. Window apertures now have continuous body frames, inset seals and glass. Rebuilt grille, lights, mirrors, split tailgate, wheel dishes and six actual rim apertures. The right wheel rotation error exposed by the rear review is fixed.

The cockpit now has a continuous dash/cowl, steering wheel, controls, separate instrument needles, readable speedometer/tachometer markings, inner door cards and adjusted seats. A simplified engine bay and underbody spare prevent obvious empty views when opening the bonnet. This is a stylized interpretation, not a verified factory reproduction or a detailed mechanical simulation.

R6/R7 refinement: replaced arch-relative body rows with clipped, level rows following the body section. This removes the irregular diagonal interpolation that made doors look dented. Refined bonnet cross sections and matched its perimeter to the wings after the first refinement render exposed a gap. Smoothed manufactured bevel shading on bumpers, mirrors and cockpit controls while retaining the existing low-poly geometry. Added bumper intake/cap details, rear bumper tread pads, mirror mounting bases, window wipes, cleaner side mouldings and a fuel-flap seam. Reviewed actual front/rear/side/cockpit images before rebaking the asset. Overall vehicle dimensions, wheels and bone/pivot positions remain consistent with the preceding asset.

## Export contract

Authoring coordinates: metres, front -Y, vehicle-left +X, Z up. Export: centimetres, front +X, left +Y before Unreal's handedness conversion. FBX armature object is `Armature`, with one `Root` and 18 rigid child bones. Do not parent mesh objects to the armature object; the modifier supplies skinning.

Bones: Root; Wheel_FL, Wheel_FR, Wheel_RL, Wheel_RR; Door_FL, Door_FR, Door_RL, Door_RR; Hood; TailgateUpper, TailgateLower; Steering; Needle_Speedometer, Needle_Tachometer, Needle_Fuel, Needle_Voltage, Needle_Coolant, Needle_Oil.

Wheelbase 285 cm, track 160 cm, wheel hubs 41.5 cm above ground, tyre radius 41.5 cm. LOD0 bounds: approximately 486.6 × 225.1 × 189.0 cm, including mirrors. These are asset measurements, not replacement values for the existing car's tuning.

| Version | Triangles | Meshes / material slots |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| LOD0, full cockpit | 47,652 | 4 / 4 |
| LOD1 | 38,696 | 4 / 4 |
| LOD2 | 32,702 | 4 / 4 |

Four slots: Exterior, Interior, Glass, Markings. Exterior and Interior each have a 2048² BaseColor PNG (sRGB) and ORM PNG (linear: R=1, G=roughness, B=metallic). R is a neutral AO channel, not baked occlusion. No lighting is baked into BaseColor. Glass requires a suitable Unreal translucent material. Markings use the LC_Colour vertex attribute for pale gauge graphics, silver lettering and dark plate text; the shared atlas initially destroyed these small details. Connect Vertex Color to Base Color for this material in Unreal. The rigged and LOD .blend files pack textures. FBX texture copies are beside the export in .fbm folders; recreate the explicit material graph in Unreal rather than trusting FBX to translate packed roughness/metallic connections.

## Verified offline

- Rendered and visually inspected front, rear and cockpit through multiple revisions, then reviewed the baked asset rather than the procedural source only.
- Finite geometry, nonzero triangle areas, UVs, exactly one full-weight bone influence per vertex.
- Each of the 18 moving groups rotated around its intended pivot and evaluated positions compared with the expected rigid transform (errors below 0.0001 m).
- Fresh-scene FBX re-imports for all three LODs: exact triangle counts, four meshes/material slots, 19 bones, UVs, weights, axes, bounds, wheelbase and track all pass.
- LOD reduction produced duplicate faces; mesh validation removes them before export, and exact round-trip counts now pass.
- R7's first globally reduced LOD2 damaged body highlights despite passing topology checks. The final distance versions preserve the exterior body panels and reduce wheels/interior only. This intentionally trades lower triangle savings for a stable body shape; their cost and switching distances still need in-engine measurement.
- Three editable convex body boxes are included in the hidden PhysicsProxies collection and described in the validation report. They are a collision fitting template, not a validated Unreal PhysicsAsset.

The 47.7k full model exceeds the first draft's arbitrary 30k modelling ceiling. The extra geometry covers improved body surfaces, actual rim apertures and a complete visible cockpit; use measured in-engine cost to decide the final budget. R7 adds 2,664 triangles over R5, principally from the more consistent body layout and manufactured details. LOD switching distances are not yet chosen. Lower LODs are for external distance views, not the driver's camera.

## Rebuild commands

Run with Git Bash from the project root:

```sh
bash Art/rebuild_landcruiser.sh R7 front,rear,cockpit
bash Art/package_landcruiser.sh --source R7 --package Package_v03
bash Art/validate_landcruiser.sh --package Package_v03
bash Art/review_landcruiser_package.sh --package Package_v03
python Art/Blender/finalize_landcruiser_rebuild.py --package Package_v03
```

These regenerate versioned files; preserve manual edits before rerunning. The old finalizer targets rejected iteration 05 and must not be used for the new model.
