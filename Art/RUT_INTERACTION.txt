On the landscape surface actor, Use Rut Interaction enables this pass. It is
false by default, so previously approved maps retain their behaviour. Disable
it before Play for a control comparison. The layer-depth and bearing-strength
settings are unchanged: standard 1/1, firm .65/1.6, deep 1.28/.6.

WHAT CHANGED
Rolling transports displaced soil preferentially into a low bow ahead of the
tyre. Positive driven slip moves the deposit toward the heel; reverse driving
reverses the transport direction. World-position clod variation breaks up the
shoulders. Wet newly deposited banks relax into a lower adjacent mud cell,
outside the loaded footprint. Both the cut and these transfers conserve volume.
The existing finite mineral floor, load settlement, shear law and water remain.

The physics thread uses the existing immutable 9 x 9 contact-height snapshot.
Three probes across the footprint measure the rise into each direction of
motion. The rolling toe subtracts the clearance afforded by the circular tyre;
the sidewall encounters the actual lateral rise. Added work opposes entry into
that material. Wet worked banks yield more readily. There is no centreline
attractor, spring, speed cap or extra force driving the car downhill. Longitudinal
and lateral impulses each have a supported-mass stopping bound. Existing soil
drag remains separately bounded. Actual heights handle authored and player ruts.

The first live run exposed too much forward resistance from counting ground
the circular tyre could clear. That run was rejected; its reports are preserved
in Saved/RutInteraction-FirstReview. The revised geometry-aware response was
rebuilt and rerun. Only fresh clean-exit passing evidence is packaged for review.

SCALING
No new global field, world scan, collision mesh cook or rendering pass. Deposits
and wet-bank relaxation only visit the current stamp neighbourhood. Terrain
observation remains capped at 30 Hz; physics probes use the existing local copy.
The water solver consumes the existing changed-bed journal. No additional
particle system or second water simulation was added.

RutInteractionReport reports actual physics-thread bank work in joules, peak
force in newtons, and mean/peak calculation time per active wheel step. Counters
include the vehicle's initial spawn, so the driving harness subtracts pre-drive
work. GetSurfaceReport now includes observation mean/peak CPU time for all wheel
queries and stamps. Rendered frame metrics are separate from these CPU timings.
The added lateral_j counter separates sidewall work from rolling-toe work.
The steered tyre axis comes from the actual Chaos wheel steering angle, rather
than legacy travel-direction data. The review applies steering through the
car's SetRawSteer path and verifies actual front-wheel angles, avoiding the
car tick overwriting a one-off movement-component steering input.

LIMITS
Local relaxation occurs while tyres work the soil; after-departure slumping,
erosion and sediment transport are not implemented. The model is an engineering
approximation, not MudRunner source or a calibrated terramechanics model. Driving
feel still needs the owner's review. The separate comparison maps are not yet a
combined material-transition route. Whole-world and multiplayer limits from
MUD_WATER_SHARED.txt still apply.
