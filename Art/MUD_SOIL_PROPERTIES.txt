SOIL PROPERTIES -- 2026-10-13

Edit the GTLandscapeSurface actor, Landscape Surface > Soil Properties, before Play.
These controls apply to the opted-in Soil Shear Response proving ground. Default
values preserve the baseline the owner liked. Restart Play after changing them;
live rebasing of an already deformed soil cache is deliberately not supported.

Layer Depth Scale: multiplies the painted yielding layer below the reference
plane, without enlarging the clods. 1 gives approximately 33-39 cm in the bog;
1.28 gives approximately 42-50 cm. The mineral base and renderer currently stop
at 50 cm below that plane. Existing hollows consume part of the available layer.

Bearing Strength: 1 is the baseline, .6 is weak bog, 1.6 is firmer soil. Reducing
it increases load-dependent equilibrium sinking AND the compressed tyre bed.
Depth alone is a capacity, not a command to bury every tyre: strong deep soil can
still carry the car. The wheel-size support safety limit also bounds immersion.
Existing contact resistance reacts to the increased immersion; no speed limit
or per-preset speed multiplier is added.

Settlement Speed Scale: changes how quickly the additional loaded penetration
approaches equilibrium (1 baseline), not the final depth. Plastic ruts retain
the existing bounded pressure-update rate. Wetness/cohesion in Mud Regions
still control soil shear and remoulding; they are independent of layer depth.

WATER INTEGRATION -- ISOLATED PROTOTYPE
The soil comparison maps retain their masked SingleLayerWater pool. A separate
/Game/GrandTour/ArtTests/MudWater/L_MudWater now connects this soil to the shared
GTWaterField simulation. See Art/MUD_WATER_SHARED.txt for controls, validation,
scaling limits and the review. The comparison maps have not been migrated.

The integration requirements were:
1. Feed the live mud bed into the local water interaction window, including
   fresh ruts and displaced banks. Apply bounded dirty-region updates; do not
   rebuild the full landscape or fluid domain for each wheel stamp.
2. Let that water solver own surface elevation, depth and velocity. Render the
   same result over mud, remove the duplicate proving-ground pool, and route
   wheel/hull samples through the existing shared water query. No added second
   buoyancy/drag path. Current GTWaterSubsystem registers legacy mud patches,
   not this new LandscapeSurface; that missing connection must be explicit.
3. Transfer wheel/hull displacement into the solver once. Drive muddy splash,
   clearer water spray and tyre washing from shared depth/current/contact data.
4. Verify bed-change mass accounting, dry/wet shore movement, waves entering
   newly connected ruts, no force duplication, and bounded update cost. Compare
   the existing Benmore water before/after; keep the current soil baseline.

Validation: Art/mud_proving_ground.ps1 -Mode PropertiesReview captures matched
standing-load and driven-track views and actual vehicle telemetry. The report
is Art/Export/MudProvingGround/propertiesreview-report.json. Rendered evidence
and measured settlement must be checked before reporting the comparison ready.
