SHARED MUD WATER -- 2026-10-13

Play /Game/GrandTour/ArtTests/MudWater/L_MudWater.
This is an isolated copy of the accepted proving ground. Soil thickness,
bearing strength, relief and tyre-contact model keep the accepted settings.
The Benmore river and the soil property comparison maps are not replaced.

The pond uses GTWaterSheet, GTWaterField and GTWaterSubsystem, the existing
water framework. The river evolves disturbances around its authored current;
this pond uses the same field's conservative wet/dry solver with sealed edges.
Wheel and hull occupancy use the existing displacement path. The rendered
surface elevation and normal come from the simulated field, not a second
cosmetic wave animation. The original flat proving-ground pool is removed
only in L_MudWater.

The bed is the original landscape plus live mud relief. A cut keeps the water
already present in that cell; it does not refill to a fixed water table. Water
can flow from connected cells into a newly opened rut, while displaced soil
can push water out. Fine mud heights clip the rendered shoreline and limit
the physical water column. Buried tyre/hull volume underneath the soil is not
counted as an extra depth of water. Muddy water keeps kind Rut for dirt/coating
consumers, with an explicit flag allowing the existing hull forces to read a
real simulated pond. Legacy cosmetic rut water remains excluded from hull lift.

SCALING AND CONTROLS
On MudWater_SharedPond: Mud Half Size Cm sets the connected basin extent;
Mud Cell Cm requests resolution. Side length is capped at 128 cells. The test
uses 126 x 126 cells at 25 cm, covering 31.5 x 31.5 m. Narrower ruts are resolved
by the fine mud mask; their water flow is still limited by the water grid.
Use a smaller basin for finer fluid cells rather than scaling the grid without
a budget. Base Level Cm sets initial fill before Play; it is not a live refill.
Restart Play after editing these controls. The sheet's Mud Surface and the
ground actor's Simulated Water references must point to one another. A sheet
should enclose the whole connected basin; its outer edges are sealed.

gt.Water.Mud.MaxActive defaults to 1, supports 0..4. Selection is one linear
world scan per frame, retaining only the nearest candidates. Only nearby
selected ponds step; sleeping initialized ponds keep water and deformation.
Bed geometry is updated at most 10 Hz from changed 2 m ground blocks, with a
sampling halo. Field texture upload is capped at 30 Hz. Existing fixed fluid
substeps and catch-up limits are retained and instrumented.

This bounds active simulation work. It is NOT yet an unlimited-world paging
system: visited ponds retain CPU/GPU state; distant unvisited ponds need a
static display for production, and adjoining domains need explicit exchange
rather than overlapping sheets. Multiplayer focus selection, save/load of
fluid state, rainfall/infiltration, erosion and river-to-pond water exchange
are not implemented in this prototype. Splash particles remain a separate
visual layer fed by the shared contact data. No full 3D fluid claim is made.

REPRODUCTION

as a separate command and require exit 0. Never close another editor by force.

Evidence is in Art/Export/MudWaterShared/shared-mud-water.html and review-report.json.
The native test opens a dry bank, verifies wave blocking, volume conservation
and wetting of the cut. The scene checks actual vehicle occupancy, moving-bed
updates, water depth/current, shader compilation, sleeping and waking, and
clamps. CPU solver timings exclude renderer cost; screenshot captures pause
simulation and cannot establish a whole-game frame-rate target.

SETUP CORRECTION
The first setup used save_map to duplicate the baseline but did not switch
the active editor world. The added sheet and pool replacement therefore went
into the source map. They were removed and the accepted pool was restored
using its transform, mesh and material from the untouched pre-edit clone.
Art/Export/MudWaterShared/repair-report.json records this recovery and its backups.
Setup now explicitly loads the copy and verifies that the baseline file hash
does not change. Repair mode is a one-time recovery tool, not a normal step.
