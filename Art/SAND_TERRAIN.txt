SAND TERRAIN — isolated drivable comparison

Open /Game/GrandTour/ArtTests/SandTerrain/L_SandTerrain.
The PlayerStart faces +X in the loose lane at Y=-280 cm. The compacted lane
is alongside at Y=+350 cm (to the right when facing +X). Drive along either
lane, stop, pull away and cross between them. The change is gradual.

Loose: 38 cm available soil layer; softness approximately 0.88–0.97.
Compacted: 12 cm layer; softness approximately 0.22–0.29.
Layer thickness is available material, not a promised wheel sink depth.
Loaded equilibrium depends on weight, softness, existing relief and the
mineral base. Both use the actual Chaos wheel support path.

The opted-in granular law separates grip, lateral grip and rolling work
from wet clay. Compressed sand becomes firmer; excess wheelspin loses
traction. Local displaced material spreads into wider shoulders and
relaxes toward a shallower bank angle while the tyre is working it.
Cut and deposited volumes remain equal. This is a bounded contact model,
not a simulation of every grain or of after-departure dune avalanches.

The material uses a unique physical height field, broad colour variation,
fine distance-filtered grain normals and a light directional tread mark.
Numeric paint/rest textures are saved linear, uncompressed and without
mips, and checked again in the fresh editor used for review.
The scene uses existing dry wheel dust. Wet mud flicks and tyre mud coating
are disabled for this dry study. No water surface is present.

Surface controls: Use Granular Response, Use Soil Shear Response and Use
Rut Interaction are all enabled here. Layer Depth Scale changes the
available layer; Bearing Strength changes yielding under load;
Settlement Speed Scale changes the rate of settlement. Granular response
defaults off, preserving the accepted mud maps.

Rebuild numeric data:
  UE bundled Python: Art/tools/make_sand_terrain.py
Before each build/editor invocation, run separately:
  powershell -NoProfile -File Art/wait_unreal_free.ps1 -MaxMinutes 0
Compile C++:
  powershell -NoProfile -File Art/build_guarded.ps1
Then run (with a separate preflight before each):
  powershell -NoProfile -File Art/sand_terrain.ps1 -Mode Setup
  powershell -NoProfile -File Art/sand_terrain.ps1 -Mode Tests
  powershell -NoProfile -File Art/sand_terrain.ps1 -Mode Review
Package verified native captures:
  UE bundled Python: Art/tools/make_sand_terrain_review.py

Review: Art/Export/SandTerrain/sand-terrain.html
Live measurements: Art/Export/SandTerrain/review-report.json
Process validation: *-run-status.json in the same directory.
Native suite: GrandTour.LandscapeMud (12 tests including GranularSand),
GrandTour.Mud.CarMud and GrandTour.DustLab.Emission (14 total).
Runtime checks cover sinkage separation, traversal, granular contact,
dryness, volume conservation, dust emission, absence of wet mud coating
and finite car positions.
Frames are short local editor runs capped at 60 fps, not a whole-map
scalability guarantee. Existing active tiles/upload budgets are reused.

Original L_RutInteraction and M_ProvingSoil files are hash-protected during
setup. Assets are private to SandTerrain. No Benmore deployment or commit.
Earlier rejected review report/logs are retained under
Saved/SandTerrain/FirstReview; these are not the packaged final evidence.
