# Hard points

Car frame: metres, X lateral, front = -Y, Z up, origin mid-wheelbase on the ground. Angles in
degrees. From `docs/CAR_GRAMMAR_PLAN.md` section 2, one name per row (grouped names such as
"WB, TRACK_F, TRACK_R" are split; each keeps the group's meaning).

| Name | Meaning | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| WB | wheelbase | m |
| TRACK_F | track, front | m |
| TRACK_R | track, rear | m |
| TYRE_R | tyre radius | m |
| TYRE_W | tyre width | m |
| OH_F | overhang from axle to bumper face, front | m |
| OH_R | overhang from axle to bumper face, rear | m |
| W_BODY | body width at the doors, no mirrors, no flares | m |
| W_FLARE | width over the flares | m |
| W_ROOF | roof width between the rail rounds | m |
| H_SILL | body lower edge at the doors | m |
| H_BELT | belt line (bottom of the side glass) at the doors | m |
| H_COWL | windscreen base at the centre line | m |
| H_BONNET_F | bonnet leading edge | m |
| H_ROOF | roof top at the centre | m |
| H_BUMPER_T | bumper bar top | m |
| H_BUMPER_B | bumper bar bottom | m |
| H_LAMP | headlamp centre | m |
| Y_COWL | windscreen base at the centre | m |
| Y_A_BASE | A-pillar base at the belt | m |
| Y_ROOF_F | roof front edge (windscreen top) | m |
| Y_ROOF_R | roof rear edge (rear screen top) | m |
| Y_RS_BASE | rear screen base | m |
| Y_B | B-pillar centre line at the belt | m |
| Y_C | C-pillar centre line at the belt | m |
| Y_D | D-pillar centre line at the belt | m |
| RAKE_WS | windscreen rake from vertical | deg |
| RAKE_RS | rear screen rake from vertical | deg |
| RAKE_FRONT | front face rake from vertical | deg |
| RAKE_REAR | tail face rake from vertical | deg |
| TUMBLEHOME | side glass lean from vertical | deg |
| ARCH_R | arch opening radius | m |
| ARCH_TOP_FLAT | flat length at the top of a squared arch | m |
| GROUND_CLEAR | underbody clearance | m |
