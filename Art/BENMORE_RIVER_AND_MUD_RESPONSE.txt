PHYSICS SEAM / LIMITS
The MudResponse study opts into EnableWheelSupport. QueryWheelSurface verifies that
the contact belongs to this landscape, then samples its displaced height across the
tyre footprint. The existing vehicle tuning/radius path applies a signed, rate-limited
support offset (maximum 12 cm, with an additional wheel-size limit for sinking).
This lets crowns lift the suspension and ruts lower it without double-counting legacy
sinkage. Loaded tyres also penetrate the soft soil independently of permanent rut
compression: 7 cm at full softness and nominal load, reduced by compaction and grass,
with an 8 cm maximum and a hard stop at the painted mineral base. Penetration settles
at 2-5 cm/s and unloads at 8 cm/s; it resets outside supported mud. It does not stamp
extra permanent depth into every pass. The same sample supplies soft-ground friction and modest rolling resistance;
compressed ground reduces the added plough resistance.

This is a height-support approximation, not rebuilt landscape collision: collision
normals, belly collision and horizontal rut-wall forces still use the original ground.
It needs subjective driving review. EnableWheelSupport defaults off elsewhere;
the setup script enables it only on L_MudResponse. Legacy mud-patch throw/coordinator
code remains intact. Water spray is a future adaptation, not changed.

Packing budgets are soft: one 32x32 block may overshoot. Override the balanced
base budget with gt.LandscapeMud.UploadBudgetMs (0.1..8, low x0.5 / high x2).
The cap on pending render updates prevents unbounded buffering when the GPU falls
behind. New wheel contact changes are uploaded before old wetness decay. Cells
outside disturbed areas are no longer scanned each frame. Settled cosmetic cells
retire from the decay set; persistent height/track data remains.

Full texture windows are no longer packed twice at 20 Hz. Dirty 32x32 regions are
uploaded instead. A moving window refresh is spread across frames into staging
textures; the old origin remains bound until the complete replacement is queued.
Four 768x768 RGBA16F textures use about 18 MiB total (including staging), independent
of world size. The CPU tile cache remains bounded conservatively at 128 MiB.
CPU tile initialization is still synchronous; long-distance streaming needs a
separate sustained drive check. This is not a whole-world streaming certification.

Spray uses persistent instance buffers and batched transforms, rather than clearing
and recreating every instance every frame. Only descending clods trace for landing.
Low/medium clods do not cast shadows. Low retains scanned normals and geometric mud
relief, but drops parallax/fine spray. Landscape shadow cache invalidation now follows
actual soil geometry changes at the preset frequency; high retains continuous refresh.
